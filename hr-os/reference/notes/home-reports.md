# home-reports notes (HR OS Home, Me and Reports)

Built 2026-11-04 by the home-reports builder. 17 operations, all reads, in `cat_home_core.js` (4: `home.admin`, `home.manager`, `home.me`, `home.directory`) and `cat_reports_core.js` (13: `reports.list`, `reports.headcount`, `reports.movement`, `reports.tenure`, `reports.span`, `reports.diversity`, `reports.comp-bands`, `reports.cost`, `reports.leave-liability`, `reports.absence`, `reports.hiring`, `reports.training`, `reports.export`). `reports.export` is people only (`agent: 'deny'`, CSV, audited); the rest allow your AI agent. Migration `1730008000_reports.js` (`report_cache`), cron `cron_reports.js` (`reports_warm`, every 10 minutes). No events, no job kinds.

## Files

- `pb/pb_hooks/lib_reports_cache.js` (the company figures that need the time and leave engines for everyone: absence per person and month, leave balances per person and day, kept in `report_cache` with a fingerprint of their inputs, the warm-up job), `cron_reports.js`, `pb/pb_migrations/1730008000_reports.js`.
- `pb/pb_hooks/lib_home.js` (the four home reads), `lib_reports.js` (filters, the population on a day with its job record, namer, FX, headcount, movement, tenure, span, diversity with suppression, the catalog, export), `lib_reports_pay.js` (cost, grade ranges, leave liability), `lib_reports_ops.js` (absence and overtime, hiring funnel, training), `cat_home_core.js`, `cat_reports_core.js`, `guide_reports.js`.
- Company globe and even panels (2026-11-10): `pb/pb_hooks/lib_home_globe.js` (the `globe` part of home.admin), `lib_home_geo.js` (country and region centres), `components/home/globe.tsx` (the section: Globe | Office switch, readouts, tooltips, lazy load), `globe3d.ts` and `office3d.ts` (the two three.js scenes, each its own chunk), `scene3d.ts` (what they share: tokens as colours, theme watch, disposal; three.js lands in its chunk), `even.tsx` (the even panels). Dependencies `three`, `topojson-client`, `world-atlas` (and their `@types`) in `dependencies`.
- Frontend: `pages/home/index.tsx` (Home per role), `pages/me/{index,Home,Pages,parts}.tsx` (Me hub, my details, documents, requests, directory, ask, and the employee Home), `pages/reports/{index,Views}.tsx` (hub and the eleven report pages), `components/home/{parts,types}.ts(x)`, `components/reports/{charts,parts}.tsx`, `i18n/{home,me,reports}.en.ts`.
- Tests `reference/tests/home-reports/`: `t01_empty.mjs` (149 checks: signed out, empty company as owner and your AI agent, one manager and one employee, CSV of every report), `t02_seeded.mjs` (on `seed_reports.mjs`, including the kept figures against a fresh count and a closed month seen at once after a change), `t03_scale.mjs` (300 people through operations as QA's scale seed, every home and report read timed; median under 2 s), `seed_reports.mjs`, `seed_empty.mjs`, `shots.mjs`, `probe.mjs` (timings), `t04_globe.mjs` (the globe data on the seeded company, after t02).

## Decisions

- Loading (UI round, 2026-11-05): each page sends ONE combined read while it renders (`components/home/load.tsx` `usePageOp`, shared while in flight): `home.admin` carries the setup steps, the pay runs of every schedule (`payroll.home`) and, for a manager, the team board; `home.manager` the team board (`attendance.board`) and the clock (`clock.status`, the whole answer: the clock card punches with it, so the page sends no clock read of its own); `home.me` the clock and the latest requests (`approvals.list`), and with `places: true` (the Me page) the module facts and the Me place counts (`tasks.list`, `handbook.list`, `payslips.mine`, `kiosk.pin-status`). Other modules' reads run server-side through `lib_ops.runAs(ctx, op, params)` (their permission and param checks, their handlers), only where the caller may run them. The modules' Inbox sections in Needs you come from the shell's one read of every section (kernel-frontend's `ShellCountsProvider`, `useShellCounts().items`), so Home sends no section read; the modules' widgets mount after the page's read and those sections answered, 250 ms apart, and only when they come within 200 px of the viewport (`Later`).

- Home is composed: the modules' `homeWidgets` are placed by `order` and `size` (wide 8 of 12 columns, narrow 4, a widget that renders nothing takes no room), and every module's `inboxSections` is a group of Needs you on Home. The employee Home puts widgets ordered 20 to 29 (the leave balance) right after the clock, then the lower orders, so the clock and the leave balance are the first two things on a phone.
- Needs you items that can be done in one tap carry `action: { op, params, label }` from the server (Start onboarding or offboarding runs `checklists.start`); approvals approve with `approvals.decide` (a PIN approval asks the PIN).
- The pay calendar on the admin Home reads payroll-core's `payroll.home` (per schedule, with blockers). The 12-month cost series and the cost report sum payslips of locked and paid runs by pay month in each pay currency, then convert at the month-end kernel rate (`lib_reports.toReporting` over `lib_fx.convert`; a month before the company's first rate for the pair takes the first rate known after it). On Home, when no rate to the reporting currency is known and every payslip is in one currency, the series and the tile stay in that currency (`cost_currency`, "No USD rate yet"); with several currencies the tile shows each currency's own total (`cost_month_parts`) and the chart lists what it leaves out. Never a zero for want of a rate.
- Turnover classification comes from lifecycle `separations.kind` (approved or completed): resignation and retirement voluntary, termination and end_of_contract involuntary, death and other other; a leaver without a separation is `not_recorded` (never read from the end reason text).
- Diversity: one dimension at a time, counts of distinct people; groups under 5 have `count: null` ("fewer than 5"); when exactly one group would be hidden the next smallest is hidden too; a total under 5 hides everything. Gender groups by the stored text (case-insensitive), age by birth date bands, nationality by country code.
- Grade ranges are read as yearly pay; pay records are annualised (month x12, week x52, day and hour by the schedule's days and hours a week, 5 and 40 when the person has no schedule, flagged) and salaries scaled to full time by FTE, then converted to the grade currency.
- Speed at 300 people (scale round, `t03_scale.mjs`): absence and leave liability read company figures kept in `report_cache`. Absence per person and month comes from ONE `lib_time.payInputsDetailMany` read for everyone (identical to the per-person reads) plus one read of the approved leave's day lists; balances on a day from one leave session with the ledgers preloaded (`lib_leave.session`, `preload`, `ruleFor`, `tracked`, `quickBalance`, the same calls as the leave module's own balances table). A closed month or a day's balances are used while their fingerprint matches (row counts and last changes of every input collection, and a digest of who was in work); the running month also for 20 minutes after it was counted. `reports_warm` counts the current month (and the last one in its first five days) and today's balances again every 10 minutes when their inputs changed; `fresh: true` (the "Count again" button) counts at once. Absence is counted by whole months (the period widens to the months it touches). The answers carry `counted_at`.
- Leave liability: the types paid out when someone leaves (`leave_types.payout_on_exit`, basis `payout`) by default, or every paid type (`paid`); value at annual pay / (days a week x 52) per day, / (hours a week x 52) per hour, the schedule of the job record or the entity default (read once per schedule); money only with pay access (rows carry no money fields otherwise).
- Absence and overtime: from the time module's classified days: scheduled days are days of kind work, a day marked absent counts 1, a half day without leave 0.5, overtime is the parts with a multiplier of 1 or more (night and similar premiums paid on top are not extra time); leave days by type as the leave module's pay inputs count them (hour types by their day equivalent). Default 3 months, at most 12.
- Hiring funnel: an application counts in every stage kind up to the furthest it reached (current stage, stage moves in `hiring_events`, hired status), by the opening's stage kinds.
- Pay visibility: `reports.cost` and `reports.comp-bands` need payroll view and, for your AI agent, `settings.agent_sees_pay`; `home.admin` carries no money when pay is hidden.
- The company directory (`home.directory`) gives work details only (name, title, department, location, entity, work email, manager), inside the member's entity scope, initials only (no photo fetch).
- Admin Home order (2026-11-10): setup guide, the company globe, Needs you beside Pay runs (or Team today for a manager without payroll) at one height (the partner sets the row height from lg up and Needs you scrolls inside it; alone it takes its own height), People and cost, then the even panels. The admin readouts strip is gone (the globe's readouts carry those figures). The modules' admin `homeWidgets` are no longer placed on the admin Home (manager and employee Homes still place theirs).
- Company globe (`globe` in home.admin): one office per entity, or per location when one entity is in scope (people without a location stand at the entity); coordinates from the location, else the region or country centre; in now, remote and on leave from the time module's board per entity (each entity's own today; without time access in now and remote are null and on leave comes from approved leave); clock-ins of the last 30 minutes pulse; open roles by entity (and location); next pay day from the pay panel (payroll access); payroll this month = gross plus employer of calculated, approved, locked and paid runs paying this month, converted at today's rate (each currency's own total when a rate is missing; pay access only). Head office: the first entity (or the largest location).
- The globe scene: land dots from world-atlas 110m (antimeridian rings unwrapped), --hr-* colours read live, day and night from the real sun position, pillars by headcount split in now / remote / on leave / not in, arcs from the head office with particles that speed up toward pay day, drag with inertia, hover eases to an office and shows the tooltip, a click (or second tap) opens its people (entity: the sidebar switch; location: the People filter). Reduced motion: no auto-turn, particles or rings, frames only on change. The loop runs only while on screen and the page is visible; DPR at most 2; the chunk loads after Home paints; without WebGL only the readouts show. 60 fps measured (GTX 970, 1440 and 390 at DPR 3).
- Office view (2026-11-11, `office3d.ts`, `floors` in the globe answer): one single-storey cutaway building per entity (open roof) on a shared ground like a small campus, orthographic isometric camera, on a grid that reads across the screen (4 across on desktop, 2 on phones, head office first, at most 8, the rest on a "+N more offices" plate; an office over 2.2 times the median size is drawn smaller), rooms per department behind low glass partitions, one desk per person in name order (so desks keep their places). States: in now (seated figure, screen on), remote (empty chair, teal lamp), on leave (chair tucked in, amber card), late and not in (dim desk), unknown without Time access. Someone who clocked in within 6 minutes walks in from the lobby to the desk. Each floor's light follows its local hour (daylight in the glass and on the floor, warm lamps in the evening, darker at night; window glass blocks the sun after dark). Rows grow with each office's people; four rooms or more sit in two bands along a corridor. Instanced meshes per floor (desk, chair, person, screen, bulb, lamp pool, leave card, leaves); structure merged per floor with edge lines; shadows from a low key light, updated only while something moves that casts them. Drag turns (about 25 degrees), wheel or pinch zooms to 1.7 (the page scrolls at either end); hover lifts an office with a tooltip placed beside it, never under the pointer; a desk tooltip shows the person; a click opens the entity's people or the profile. Name plates say country and local time. The view choice is kept in localStorage (`hr.home.view3d`). 60 fps measured (GTX 970) on the demo, the owner's mock company (8 offices) and 300 people on one floor, 1440 and 390 at DPR 3. `home.admin` at 300 people: 354 to 453 ms.
- Even panels (`even.tsx`): two to a row (one on phones), body six rows of 40 px, one "See all" each: Your AI agent (status, latest work, the ask box; always, and alone it takes the row before anyone is in), Team today (admins who manage people, with pay view), Who is out (today and later this week), Starting and leaving (30 days, checklist progress), Open roles (candidates by stage), Expenses (waiting, flagged, to pay), and Reports only to make the count even. Praise (little use on an admin Home) and the All reports bar were dropped; approvals stay in Needs you only. The grid's `keep` keeps an odd last panel at half width.
- Setup guide: the kernel's setup steps (`lib_settings.progress`, sent inside `home.admin` as `setup` to admins) until every step is done, then gone (no dismissal). The open steps come first in the order people, departments, team, PIN, company; the first one leads with its action, and while nobody is in that is the kernel's `<FirstPeople inline />` (pages/people/Start.tsx: Hire and Import a spreadsheet). Without the guide (not an admin) and nobody in, `<FirstPeople />` stands alone for whoever may add people; readouts, the chart and the widgets wait for the first people.
- A sign-in without an employee record (the owner who set the company up): Me, the Me pages and the employee Home render the kernel's `<NotLinked />` (an administrator links the sign-in to their person or adds themselves; anyone else is told who links it). No module read runs for such a sign-in (the kiosk PIN status answered 409 before).
- Home grid (`components/home/grid.tsx`, all three Homes): Home's own places and the modules' widgets in one list. No dense packing: each row of 12 columns (lg up) is filled by stretching its last place (`packRows`), so the order on screen is the page order at every width. A widget that renders nothing for the viewer takes no place and no number (seen by a MutationObserver on its wrapper). Numbers go to the visible places in that order once the page has settled (its read in, every widget mounted, no change for 300 ms; marker `data-settled`), never while loading. Widgets mount once the page's read is in, 120 ms apart (no lazy mount: lower widgets must count). Each widget gets `index` (`HomeWidgetProps`). `tests/home_numbers.mjs` checks 01..N in visual order at 360, 390 and 1440.
- Facts on Me are the kernel's `ProfileFact` drawn with the kernel's `FactValue` (money through `Money`, so Hide pay applies; `date`, `days`, `hours`, `count` formatted).
- Clock card: tapping shows the new state at once (Home's `home.me` and `home.manager` views reshaped, the time module's `punchNow` saves and offers Undo through `clock.undo`; a refused punch puts the old state back). Needs you row actions are default buttons (one primary per screen: the clock, or the setup guide's next step).
- A manager without reports (or without an employee record) gets an empty board and their approvals; Team today says "Nobody reports to you yet".
- Team today follows the time board: lateness through `minutes()`, "Shift over" for an untracked person whose shift ended (`shift_over`). The clock card keeps its button on one row at one width for every punch.

## Known gaps

- Counting the company figures is slow at 300 people on a loaded machine (absence 3 months about 19 s cold, one month 3 to 6 s; balances about 3 s): the warm-up job does it in the background, a report waits only when nobody looked for a while after a change in a closed month, for another day's balances, or on "Count again".
- The leave module has no documented batch contract for balances; liability uses its exported session functions (as its own balances table does). A `lib_leave.balancesMany(app, ids, day)` contract would be cleaner.
- Required-training compliance is the performance module's page (`/performance/training`); the training report covers session completion only.
- Payroll-core's `payroll_next` admin widget repeats the first row of Home's per-schedule pay runs panel.
- Me shows a module's date fact as text until the module sends `kind: 'date'` (payroll's next pay day still sends `'day'`; the kernel's contract is `date`).

## Needs and findings for other builders

00. Deploy (2026-11-10): `frontend/package.json` gained `three`, `topojson-client`, `world-atlas`, `@types/three`, `@types/topojson-client`. The runner's install step is skipped when `frontend/node_modules` exists, so the deploy must run `npm install --no-audit --no-fund --ignore-scripts` in the live `frontend/` (or the build fails on the missing modules).
01. leave, lifecycle, hiring, expenses, performance: your `for: 'admin'` homeWidgets are no longer placed (the admin Home shows its own even panels reading `leave.home`, `lifecycle.home`, `hiring.home`, `expenses.home`); drop them when convenient.

0. Speed, platform and kernel (UI round): parallel requests are what make Home slow: with PocketBase's default hooks pool the server serves about two requests at once on warm runtimes and the rest on cold ones (1 to 20 s each on a loaded machine). Home now sends one read for everything it draws itself (eighteen before) and takes the modules' sections from the shell's counts; the shell's own reads (app.home, every section, the clock for the sidebar's punch) and the modules' widgets still run beside it. Fixes outside this module: kernel-frontend: a concurrency limit of two in `lib/api.ts` `op()` with the page's first read ahead; runner: `--hooksPool=60` (kernel-backend's measurement); kernel-backend: warm the module libraries at boot as the kernel ones are.

1. leave: the employee widget's header button "Request" is black next to Home's Clock in (two primaries, QA N13); make it a default button.
1a. lifecycle: `MoversWidget`, `MoversWidgetNarrow` and `FirstDaysWidget` do not take `index` (`HomeWidgetProps`), so Home shows them without a number (the grid leaves them out of the count, no gap); pass it to their Panel.
1b. payroll-out: the admin widget `filings_due` lists the same filings Needs you already shows from its Inbox section (QA N12); drop the widget or keep it to the next deadline only.
1c. expenses, hiring, payroll-out: widget aside links are sentence case ("Open", "Hiring", "All filings", "All openings") where Home's and the others are mono caps (QA HR6).
2. lifecycle: the manager `lifecycle.movers` widget is exported `narrow` but lays out two columns, so names wrap one letter per line at 4 of 12 columns; make it `wide` for managers or one column when narrow.
3. leave: a `balancesMany(app, employmentIds, day)` contract (see gaps).
4. kernel-frontend: `Money` with the symbol display shows SGD and USD both as "$"; report tables use `display: 'code'` for mixed pay currencies.
5. hiring: `reference/tests/hiring/seed_hiring.mjs` stops at `candidates.resume-set` (403 not_permitted: "Files inside the app's own folder cannot be attached"), so its demo data after the candidates (interviews, offers, referral) is missing on a fresh seed.
6. lifecycle and payroll-core (QA K12): their admin widgets "Starting and leaving" and "Next pay day" draw an empty or sunk panel on an empty company; give each an empty state with one action on the plain panel surface.
