# packs-asia: rule packs and country data (JP, SG, IN, MY, metadata countries)

Built 2026-11-04. 19 pack files (14 pack codes; jp and sg have 2026 and 2027 versions) in `pb/pb_hooks/packs/` and 12 country files in `pb/pb_hooks/data/countries/`. All 97 packs-asia vectors pass on the engine runner (`node tools/packs/test.mjs --hooks pb/pb_hooks`); the whole APP run is 38/38 packs, 571/571 vectors. Extra tests in `reference/tests/packs-asia/`: `integration.mjs` (packs run together: EPF feeds PCB, PF and PT feed old-regime TDS, jp with jp-yea in a December year-end run, SG with a self-help fund) and `crosscheck_jp.mjs` (the electronic 甲 rule stays within 100 yen of the official 月額表 甲欄 at every row for 0 to 7 dependants, 1,848 and 1,824 points). Generators: `reference/notes/packs-asia-gen/` (`node build.mjs` from a folder that has `../pb_hooks`; `extract_jp.py` reads the NTA and 協会けんぽ Excel files, which are not kept here).

Ship rule followed: `active` only where every number comes from an official source fetched this session (or an official page the research notes quote) and the vectors pass; everything else is `draft` with the unverified items named in `notes`.

Employee shares drop 50 sen or less and raise over 50 sen (協会けんぽ table note); health and care are rounded together as the official table does.
jp 2027.1 holds the 令和9年分 tables (pay from 1 January 2027; the 1 percent defence surtax sits inside the unchanged 102.1 percent). Its social insurance runs to February 2027 premiums and employment insurance to wage closing dates before 1 April 2027; after that the pack raises blockers (`jp_rates_unpublished`, `jp_ei_rates_unpublished`) until a 2027.2 version adds the FY2027 rates.
Not covered: 日額表 (weekly, daily, semi-monthly pay: blocker), retirement income, 子ども・子育て拠出金 and 労災 (employer only), part-time coverage tests, leaving-month premium rules, prefecture minimum wages, the 2027 pension grade 33.
