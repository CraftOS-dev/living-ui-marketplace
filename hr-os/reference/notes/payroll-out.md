## Decisions

- Bank numbers: unsealed only in `lib_payroll_out.payments(…, plain=true)` and handed straight to the writer; the plain values are blanked right after the bytes exist. Stored `items`, problems, audit and answers carry masked numbers. Files are written with `$filesystem.fileFromBytes(<JS byte array>)` (Shift_JIS bytes survive; verified by hash).
- A file is written only when every payment is valid; otherwise 422 `bank_file_problems` with `data.problems` per person. `payout.run` dry-runs the writer so the page shows problems before anyone types a PIN.
- Layouts verified this session: Nacha ACH file details (achdevguide.nacha.org), ABA (Bank of Queensland direct entry specification, CRLF, balancing record), Zengin (a 信用金庫 全銀 format page plus the notes' regional bank spec; 使用可能文字 from bank help pages: capitals, digits, kana without small kana, space and ( ) . - / , \ 「 」; ー written as -). SEPA from the ISO 20022 pain.001.001.03 structure and EPC rules (NOTPROVIDED when no debtor BIC). Bacs CSV, SG GIRO, India NEFT, Maybank and CIMB are HR OS's documented layouts labelled "check with your bank" (no public layout; Maybank's guide timed out). EBCDIC Zengin is not offered.
- Run paid: through payroll-core's `lib_payroll_runs.markPaid` only; payroll-out never writes `payruns`.
- Reconciliation (UI round, QA PO1): `payout.run`, `payout.overview` and `bankfiles.export` carry `totals`: net pay of the run, less held pay (never in a bank file), plus holds released into the run, equals to the bank plus cash and cheques (`balanced`). The run page tiles show To the bank, Held, Cash and cheque, Net pay of the run; the "Write the bank file" dialog shows the same lines before the PIN. The overview's money column is To pay (held pay left out).
- UI rules held in payroll-out files: type scale 10.5 (mono uppercase print headings only), 12, 13, 14; money through `<Money>` (print pages too, `always`); dates and filing periods through `format.ts` (`periodLabel`: "October 2026", run numbers, or the date range; `rangeWithYear` for report headers); own links and chips 40 px on phones (`min-h-10 md:min-h-0`). Unofficial bank layouts: the tag says "check with your bank", the label only describes the layout; the file fingerprint sits in a hint. Timestamps to days through format.ts (stampDayKey, fmtStampDay); every date on a printed document in that document's entity style and time zone. One primary button per screen (Year end: the first document ready to issue; the run page: Write the bank file, then Confirm upload). The entity control is the kernel `EntityPicker` (in the body on phones). Bank format labels say what the file is; the layout internals sit behind a "File layout" hint on Pay-out settings.
- `packs.list` is compact (id, code, country, name, version, status, dates in force, vectors passed, `in_use` entities, source); `--in-use` (default true for your AI agent, false for people; `left_out` counts the rest) and `--country`; the full detail is `packs.get`.
- Filings: due rules are data keyed by filing code (the packs' `due_rule` text is for people; no text parsing). A code without a rule still gets rows, without a due day, and a person sets it (`filings.set-due`, a proposal for your AI agent). An obligation is in the ledger from the go-live day (else the first locked run's period start, else today): one due before it was the earlier system's and is left out; a period that ended before it is listed only while its payment is still ahead and the entity had people then (October's remittances on a November install), never as late. When a choice changes (`filing_prefs`, or a pay frequency), the open rows of the other choice that nobody worked on (todo, no manual due day) are removed for that year; worked rows stay. Periods starting within the next 31 days are listed ahead. The ledger builds itself: `entity.created`, `payrun.locked`, `payrun.voided`, `payrun.paid`, the hourly cron (reminders once a day), and `filings.list` fills an entity that has no rows. `filings.list --show due --days N` (default 30; rejected rows always shown).
- Voided runs: rows made for that run (per pay date, per bonus) become `not_needed` with a note naming the run and its replacement (the replacement gets its own rows when it locks); rows already submitted become `amend`. Period rows recompute without the void run.
- US: `us_941_deposit` (HR OS builtin) next to the quarterly 941: the lines filed under 941, by the entity's deposit schedule (`filing_prefs.us_941_deposit` monthly, the default, due the 15th of the next month; or semiweekly, per pay date: Wednesday to Friday pay days the next Wednesday, Saturday to Tuesday the next Friday; next business day). AU `au_super` from the 2026-27 pack: 7 days after each pay day (payday super).
- `check_rule_updates`: one job per country with only the packs some entity uses (versions in force or later), each with its sources (`read_by_builder` day) and `last_checked` (the last finished check job for the country, '' never); `packs.check-updates` and `packs.ask-update` answer a brief (id, kind, pack count), not the payload. Deposits whose lines the pack files under the return (India TDS under 24Q, FUTA, UK PAYE payment, AU BAS) sum their codes (`amount` in the rule). A submitted row whose figures change becomes `amend`.
- Year-end documents read only locked or paid runs and non-void payslips; a W-2 box 3 cap uses the 2026 wage base (a warning for other years). Japanese 給与所得控除後の金額 and 所得控除 come from the 年末調整 worksheet, not repeated here.
- Pack activation re-runs validate and every vector at the moment of activation; overlapping active versions of the same code are retired. Drafts with errors are kept as drafts (the page shows why).

A company set up today with nobody gets no earlier-period remittance; once someone who worked then is hired, last month's shows while still ahead, never late.

- `t05_integration.mjs` (18): payroll-core's real Singapore and Japan runs calculated, approved and locked, then paid out (GIRO problems on real bank codes, file total equals the run net, markPaid), filings and IR8A against the real lines, Zengin from real furigana.

## Gaps and needs

- payroll-core: after Lock, the run page could offer "Write the bank file" (a link to `#/payroll/pay-out/<run>`, QA PO2); payroll-core's `pages/payroll/index.tsx` spreads `routes` and `nav` from `./out` and `routes` from `../print/payroll/out.tsx`; it should also spread `inboxSections` and `homeWidgets` from `./out` (filings due in the Inbox and on Home).
- Bank master validation (Zengin bank and branch codes, UK modulus checking) is not built: the formats check shapes and check digits only.
- Japan Post (ゆうちょ) 記号番号 conversion is a warning, not automatic.
- FX: reports leave out payslips without a rate for the pay date and list them.
- `www.w3.org` shows in the gate's egress list: it is the SEPA XML namespace string, not a request.
