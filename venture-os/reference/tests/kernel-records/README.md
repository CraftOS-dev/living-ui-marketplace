# kernel-records tests

Run against a scratch copy of the app (never the delivered folder, never a live port; the kernel-records port is 3182):

1. Copy the app to a scratch folder without `pb/pb_data`; add `stubs/` into its `pb/pb_hooks/` (the test kit: `cat_testkit.js`, `lib_testkit.js`, `events_testkit.js`; scratch only, never shipped); `frontend/node_modules/pdfjs-dist` must be installed (the text engine reads PDFs with it); run `node tools/gen.mjs` in the copy.
2. `VO_APP=<scratch copy> VO_PORT=3182 node run_all.mjs` (each test resets the scratch database through `tools/dev/reset.sh` and starts it with `tools/dev/serve.sh`).

`lib.mjs` `setupFirm` is the one place that knows the first-run params (`setup.complete`) and the invite flow; it was written against the stand-in kernel of the kernel-records scratch copy (HR OS kernel with kernel-core's dispatcher, activity tracker and catalog), so check it against the merged kernel's `setup.complete` params first. `fixtures.mjs` writes the test files by code: a two-page PDF (a text page in Helvetica and a picture-only page) and a two-slide PPTX.

Tests: t01 identity (same email twice, same root domain, co.uk suffixes, free mail), provenance and your AI agent's fill-or-propose rule (people.upsert, people.update, records.enrich, accepting a proposal), merge and undo as one unit (people, deals and the alias move back), dupes, rights; t02 deals (create with stage event and deal.created, duplicates, moves with deal.moved, status with deal.closed, agent rules: first-stage create, later stage, move, assign and update as proposals, status and restrict refused, enrich fill and proposal, founders added directly, stage_moves promoted only to Screen), restricted deals (owner, team member, outsider partner, analyst, your AI agent with and without agent_allowed, lists, board, search and company page hidden counts), pipelines (seed, stall days, spv on first use, save, stage in use), fund scope; t03 files (multipart upload, the text step of a real PDF in the background, needs_ocr on the picture page, file.added and file.text_ready, versions by name within a folder, files.version, same_as, fromPath of a downloaded attachment, --paths for your AI agent and refusals, pages.set-ocr, anchors by line, quote and message, anchors.resolve with citation scope, NDA rules for people and for your AI agent with no job, an unclaimed job, another deal's job and the right job (audited), delete as a proposal and by a person, undo of a delete and of an upload after its text step, PNG, PPTX and text files, empty files); t04 theses (FACT_KEYS is the fit engine's list, theses.keys, criteria validation, proposals, tri-state fit with exchange rates, grades, rights); t05 every read op on an empty database and with data as owner, partner, analyst and your AI agent with no 5xx, and the people-only ops refusing your AI agent before any param check.
