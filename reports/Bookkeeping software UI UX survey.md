# Agents draft the books, humans sign off

By late 2026 every serious bookkeeping product runs the same pipeline: imported statement lines and documents land in a staging inbox, ordered rules and learned suggestions turn them into **proposals labelled with how they were made** (Xero tags each automated match Rule, Match, Memory, Prediction or Manual), a person approves them in batches, and posted entries become immutable records that a statement reconciliation proves and a checklist-gated lock date freezes. Products now differ mainly in how far they trust the machine, from QuickBooks' "never without your approval" and Xero's per-account, off-by-default auto-reconciliation to Digits' claimed 95%+ untouched postings and Pilot's no-human close with no published accuracy. The interaction bar is set outside the incumbents, whose 2025 QuickBooks banking redesign drew "harder to read" complaints, by Copilot's and Actual's single-key triage, Fava's filters and error table, and Mercury's colour restraint; the rigor bar comes from plain-text accounting's exact decimals, balance assertions and MCP servers that dry-run every write. Against that standard Ledgerflow V1 is a CRUD ledger that stores money as floats and has no import, statement reconciliation, rules, audit trail, lock dates or agent, so its gaps start in storage rather than on screen. The redesign should first rebuild the data and operation layer (integer minor units, transactions with postings, a proposed/posted/voided lifecycle, payment allocations, reconciliation records, an append-only event log naming the actor) and then expose it through seven dense, keyboard-first views with the agent's work shown in an inspector tab and revertible per session. Bench's overnight shutdown in December 2024 turns Ledgerflow's local, exportable design into a selling point.

## About forty products fall into six families, and each one sets a different part of the bar

No single product is the reference design. Incumbent suites define the workflow vocabulary everyone uses: feeds, rules, reconcile, lock. ERPs define controls. AI-native ledgers define how an agent takes part. Plain-text tools define data rigor. Personal-finance apps define interaction speed and visual restraint. A Ledgerflow redesign needs something specific from each family. A caveat on sources: the help centres of FreeAgent, Monarch, Mercury and Wave blocked direct fetching, so some details below rest on search-engine excerpts of official pages. Most AI performance figures are vendor claims that no independent party has checked.

| Family | Products surveyed | What the family leads on | What Ledgerflow should take |
|---|---|---|---|

| Freelancer tools | FreshBooks, Wave, Kashoo, Bonsai | Hiding double entry behind categories; time-to-invoice | Treat each category as an account; canned entries named in plain words; batch-confirm likely matches |

### Incumbent suites rebuilt their home pages, bank feeds and invoice editors around AI

QuickBooks Online made the biggest move. Its July 2025 redesign turned the homepage into **customizable widgets plus a "business feed" that "summarizes the workflows and tasks completed by the AI agents"**. The same launch introduced a virtual team of Payments, Accounting, Finance, Customer, Marketing, Payroll and Project Management agents ([Intuit](https://investors.intuit.com/_assets/_08ee5483ec4c057568cc8774f3fd6aad/intuit/news/2025-07-01_Intuit_Introduces_Ground_Breaking_Virtual_Team_of__1258.pdf)). The new AI banking page splits downloaded lines into Pending and Posted. Vendor and category are edited inline with "no pop-up windows". Provenance is shown by a sparkle "Suggested by AI" icon, a **RULE** badge, and a **Pair** badge for transfers between the user's own accounts ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/matching-rules/learn-updates-new-ai-powered-banking-page/L0hR7A9Zf_ROW_en)). The page became the default on 1 October 2025, and the opt-out disappeared on **8 May 2026** ([Porte Brown](https://blog.portebrown.com/quickbooks-onlines-new-bank-feed-what-to-expect-in-october-2025); [Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/intuit-bank-feeds-experience-transitions-may-8-2026)). Accountants disliked the first version. One user claiming 300+ clients called it "ugly, horrible to navigate, harder to read", and Intuit had to offer a temporary revert switch ([QB Community](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/community/banking-4/new-banking-page-not-good-24079)).

Xero switched off classic invoicing on **27 February 2025**. The new editor lets users hide fields they don't need and shows a live side-by-side email preview ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/campaign/new-invoicing)). Xero also rebuilt its home page as drag-and-drop widgets, including a tasks widget for unreconciled lines and overdue invoices ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/uk/campaign/new-homepage-navigation/)). In late November 2025 its JAX agent began reconciling bank lines automatically, with an internal goal of more than 80% of statement lines ([Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/xero-rolling-out-jax-powered-automatic-bank-reconciliation)). Each bank account has its own on/off switch, and a "Reconciled" page lets users review, edit or undo any automated match ([CFOtech](https://cfotech.com.au/story/xero-rolls-out-ai-bank-reconciliation-with-jax-tool)). Xero's limit is analytical: only **two active tracking categories**, a recurring user frustration ([FieldProMax](https://www.fieldpromax.com/blog/xero-tracking-categories-for-job-costing-setup-and-limits)).

Zoho Books has the deepest keyboard layer of any suite. A Shift+? sheet lists every shortcut and lets the user edit it, Shift+digit jumps between modules, and two-key create chords such as C then I open a new invoice ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/uk/books/help/getting-started/keyboard-shortcuts.html)). Its Zia CoCreate agent fills invoices and quotes visibly on screen. The user can "Take Control" mid-fill and later "Resume Autofill", and can pick the AI provider and model ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/ai-features/ai-features.html)). Sage Accounting routes AI-captured bills into a **"Draft to approve"** queue that shows a count ([Sage KB](https://gb-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewsolution.jsp?solutionid=222001000101064)). Per a search excerpt of its product-updates page, Sage made Copilot free for all users from April 2026 ([Sage](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/sage-business-cloud/sage-accounting/product-updates/)).

FreeAgent is the quiet one. Since 2020 an ML feature called "Guess" has explained bank lines, which then wait in a **For Approval** tab in orange text with an eye icon ([FreeAgent support](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115001586004)). Its May 2025 Find & Fix tool bulk-edits up to 100 records at once and keeps a Bulk Edit History with from/to values ([FreeAgent](https://www.freeagent.com/blog/introducing-find-and-fix/)). FreeAgent states that "trained professionals need to remain accountable for the results" ([FreeAgent](https://www.freeagent.com/blog/how-freeagent-uses-ai/)). The incumbents' shared lesson is that the review queue is now the product, and that removing a dense view costs more goodwill than any missing feature.

### Freelancer tools hide double entry, while ERPs expose it and win on controls

Wave and FreshBooks keep debits and credits out of sight. In Wave a "category" is simply a chart-of-accounts account, and the chart is split into five tabs: Assets, Liabilities & Credit Cards, Income, Expense and Equity ([Wave](https://support.waveapps.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004972106)). Auto-categorized lines show a purple highlight and a thumbs-up button to confirm them ([Wave](https://support.waveapps.com/hc/en-us/articles/26387836091668)). FreshBooks lets a bank line be recorded without a source document through buttons with plain names: **Mark as Transfer, Mark as Owner's Equity and Mark as an Expense Refund**, which are effectively canned journal entries. Its "Likely Matches" list arrives with every pair pre-checked, so the user unticks bad pairs and presses Confirm and Continue ([FreshBooks](https://support.freshbooks.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039682172)). Trust is these tools' weak point. Wave moved automatic bank imports behind a $19/month Pro plan ([Merchant Maverick](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/wave-accounting-review/)), and one aggregator records a 1.2/5 Trustpilot score ([checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/wave/reviews)).

Odoo and NetSuite are openly double-entry, and they lead on controls. Every Odoo invoice, bill and payment generates a journal entry, and retained earnings are computed "without year-end rollovers" ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting.html)). Odoo 19 replaced the three-panel reconciliation widget of versions 17–18 with **one list of bank lines carrying inline suggestion buttons**: Set Partner, Set Account, Reconcile, and one button per reconciliation model. Once a row is resolved, its buttons are replaced by the entries it was matched to ([Odoo 19](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting/bank/reconciliation.html)). The same release added keyboard shortcuts to reconciliation views, a one-line description on each account saying when to use it, account pickers that rank the likely accounts first (income on invoices, expenses on bills), and an automatic "To review" flag on entries posted by users with lower access rights ([Odoo 19 release notes](https://www.odoo.com/odoo-19-release-notes)). NetSuite contributes two things. The **Period Close Checklist** is an ordered task list in which a padlock blocks any task whose prerequisites are not met ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1455781.html)). **System Notes** is an uneditable, field-level log of old and new values ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_158644279544.html)). The price is the interface: 36% of reviewers in one summary find it problematic, "outdated and clunky" ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/questions/what-needs-improvement-with-netsuite-erp)). Together the two groups point to ERP depth under a surface as simple as the freelancer tools.

### AI-native ledgers moved the agent from coding transactions to running the close

Digits launched its accounting agents on 23 June 2025 ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2025/digits-adds-ai-agents-accounting-platform/)). It describes its "agentic close" in six stages: **Collect, Book, Reconcile, Schedule, Review, Report** ([Digits](https://digits.com/product/agentic-close/)). Its help centre is unusually specific about limits: low-confidence items are never auto-posted, the review queue is framed as "a training queue, not permanent workload", the Confirm button stays disabled until a category is chosen, and the agent neither performs FX revaluation nor answers tax or legal questions ([Digits help](https://help.digits.com/firms-agentic-general-ledger/digits-ai)).

The other AI-native products each add one idea. Rillet's Aura agents produce a monthly "ready-to-review list of accruals" with "a plain-language rationale for each accrual", flux analysis with dollar and percentage materiality thresholds, and side-by-side edit diffs with plain-English summaries ([Rillet](https://www.rillet.com/blog/january-2026-product-update)). Campfire splits its agents into continuous ones (bank matching, duplicate and miscoding monitors) and on-demand ones for the close, and every answer links to GL accounts, transactions and documents ([Campfire](https://campfire.ai/ember)). Ramp's Accounting Agent lets admins grant permissions per capability (**code, review, sync**) and accepts corrections in plain language ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/accounting-agent-launch)); per a search excerpt of its admin guide, it "does not override employee values, admin values, or rule values" ([Ramp support](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/45051740591251-Ramp-Accounting-Agent-Enablement-Daily-Use-Admin-guide)). Pilot went furthest, announcing in February 2026 an AI Accountant that closes books "without human involvement" while publishing no accuracy metrics ([Pilot](https://pilot.com/blog/pilot-unveils-ai-accountant-a-major-leap-toward-artificial-general-intelligence-in-accounting)).

Bench is the cautionary tale. On **27 December 2024** it told customers the platform was immediately inaccessible, after raising over $100M ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/27/bench-shuts-down-leaving-thousands-of-businesses-without-access-to-accounting-and-tax-docs)). Customers' books lived in Bench's own proprietary system rather than QuickBooks or Xero, so exporting "in a usable format" was hard ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/328599ff-1b23-4021-9402-12ef274256de/)). Customers were told to file tax extensions, given until March 2025 to download their data, and offered no refunds ([mjtsai](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2025/01/02/bench-shut-down-then-acquired)). Employer.com bought Bench days later, and Bench filed for bankruptcy in Canada on 7 January 2025 with about $65.4M in liabilities against $2.8M in cash ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_Accounting)). For a local single-user app this risk becomes an advantage, but only if export is easy to find and uses open formats.

### Plain-text and open-source tools set the bar for rigor

Beancount (v3.2.3, May 2026) stores amounts as exact decimals ("We do not use floating-point numbers") and infers each currency's tolerance from the digits the user typed ([Beancount](https://beancount.github.io/docs/precision_tolerances/); [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/beancount/)). A transaction's postings must sum to zero in each currency. A `balance` directive asserts an account's balance at the start of a date, and `pad` inserts the entry that fills the gap to the next assertion ([Beancount syntax](https://beancount.github.io/docs/beancount_language_syntax/)). Fava, Beancount's web front end, lists every ledger problem in a sortable File / Line / Error table that links back to the source ([Fava](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/frontend/src/reports/errors/Errors.svelte)). It also puts a green, red, yellow or grey dot next to each account, depending on whether the account's last entry is a passing balance check ([Fava help](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/src/fava/help/features.md)). hledger validates the whole journal on every run, has a `--strict` mode that requires accounts and commodities to be declared, and its `recentassertions` check flags any account whose last balance assertion is more than 7 days older than its last posting ([hledger](https://hledger.org/checking-for-errors.html)).

The GUI tools add workflows on top. GnuCash 5.17 tracks each split as new, cleared or reconciled, and its import matcher offers Add, Update+Clear and Clear actions on colour-coded rows ([GnuCash](https://code.gnucash.org/docs/C/gnucash-manual/trans-import.html)). Manager.io records receipts and payments with a cleared date, parks uncategorized imports in **Suspense** behind a yellow notice ([Manager.io](https://www.manager.io/guides/bank-rules)), and can undo an entire import from its History log ([Manager.io](https://www.manager.io/guides/import-bank-statement)). Actual (v26.10) stores integer cents, has the best-documented keyboard model of any app surveyed, and does not support multiple currencies at all ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/api/reference); [Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/budgeting/multi-currency)). There are two cautionary cases: Firefly III rule failures happen silently because rules run asynchronously ([Firefly III](https://docs.firefly-iii.org/how-to/firefly-iii/features/rules/)), and Akaunting sells double entry as a paid add-on whose own docs warn that manual journals "may duplicate data in reports" ([Akaunting](https://akaunting.com/hc/docs/double-entry-accounting/creating-a-new-manual-journal/)).

### Personal-finance apps set the interaction and visual bar

Copilot marks every unreviewed transaction with a light-blue dot. On the web, arrow keys move through the list, X selects, R marks reviewed and C changes the category, and the list shows live totals for whatever filter is applied ([Copilot](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/11780342-copilot-money-for-web)). Its per-user model switches on after 30 reviewed transactions and applies a category only when confident, marking it with an "Intelligence" badge; when unsure, it pins its **top two guesses** to the top of the picker ([Copilot](https://intercom.help/copilotmoney/en/articles/8182433-copilot-intelligence-for-spending)). Monarch lets rules set a transaction's review status ([Monarch](https://www.monarch.com/blog/transaction-review)), shows a rule widget "automatically when you make any changes to a transaction anywhere", and offers to apply that rule to existing matches ([Monarch help, search excerpt](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048393372)); it added a full activity log in July 2026 and an MCP integration in October 2026 ([Monarch](https://www.monarch.com/whats-new)).

Lunch Money lets users edit any cell in place and select ranges with Shift ([Lunch Money](https://support.lunchmoney.app/finances/transactions/transaction-actions)), and its "Create & Apply" shows which transactions a new rule will change, with checkboxes to leave some out ([Lunch Money](https://support.lunchmoney.app/setup/rules.md)). Mercury reserves red and yellow "exclusively for errors and warnings" within a calm, near-monochrome palette ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/mercury-design-breakdown)) and puts bulk actions in a bar at the bottom of the screen ([Mercury, search excerpt](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/46276513379348-Categorizing-transactions)). These apps also share a weak spot that comes from pulling bank data through cloud aggregators, connections that break "weekly, sometimes daily" ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026)), which an offline app that imports files never has.

## The core workflows converge on staging, matching, proof and lock

### Import works as a staging inbox with three layers of duplicate defence

For an offline app, statement import replaces the bank feed, so it deserves the most engineering. The formats differ sharply in how much they help. **OFX/QFX** should come first: every transaction carries a FITID "used to detect duplicate downloads", and amounts are signed from the customer's point of view ([Haskell ofx docs](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/ofx-0.4.4.0/docs/Data-OFX.html)). **CAMT.053** requires opening and closing booked balances and gives each entry a bank reference that works as an idempotency key ([Mambu](https://docs.mambupayments.com/docs/camt053-xsd-business-logic)), and **MT940** carries the same balances in its `:60F:` and `:62F:` tags ([Westpac](https://bankrec.westpac.com.au/docs/statements/mt940/)). **QIF** dates are so ambiguous that practitioners say no importer can resolve them every time ([GnuCash-user](https://lists.gnucash.org/pipermail/gnucash-user/2014-April/054357.html)). **CSV** needs a column-mapping profile saved per bank; Actual's mapper adds options to flip the sign, split inflow and outflow columns, and apply a multiplier ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/transactions/importing)).

The import pipelines that work defend against duplicates in three layers. The first is an exact match on the bank's own transaction ID. The second is a hash of the normalized source row: Ledger writes a UUID checksum per CSV line ([Ledger](https://ledger-cli.org/doc/ledger3.html)), and Firefly III hashes each submitted transaction ([Firefly III](https://docs.firefly-iii.org/references/data-importer/duplicate-detection/)). The third is a fuzzy window. Beancount's beangulp compares entries within ±2 days ([beangulp](https://github.com/beancount/beangulp/blob/master/beangulp/importer.py)), GnuCash scores dates against a 4-day "likely" and a 14-day "unlikely" threshold ([GnuCash-user](https://lists.gnucash.org/pipermail/gnucash-user/2021-March/095650.html)), and Actual looks for the same amount near the same date with a similar payee, always keeping the imported version ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/transactions/importing)). hledger takes a different line. It declines to call any of this deduplication, saying "it's generally not possible to reliably detect duplicates in bank CSV", and simply remembers the latest date it has seen in each file ([hledger](https://hledger.org/hledger.html)). Monarch makes the overlap rule an explicit choice: "Prioritize CSV" replaces the existing transactions in the file's date range, and "Prioritize Monarch" only fills missing history ([Monarch help, search excerpt](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/41843105489300-Importing-Balances-and-Transactions-Manually)).

The staging step is where trust is built. Fava's importer shows each extracted entry next to the raw CSV row or XML fragment it came from, pre-ticks an "ignore duplicate" box on likely duplicates, and files the statement under the account's documents folder ([Fava import](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/src/fava/help/import.md)). beancount-import sends unknown legs to `Expenses:FIXME` and predicts them with a decision tree ([beancount-import](https://github.com/jbms/beancount-import)). Ledgerflow should store each file's hash so the same file is never imported twice, keep every line's raw source for audit and re-parsing, give identical same-day lines an occurrence index (two €3.50 coffees on one day are legitimate, and re-importing an overlapping file reproduces the same index), and turn any closing balance a statement supplies into a suggested reconciliation checkpoint.

### Matching and statement reconciliation are separate jobs

Bank-line review takes several distinct shapes. Each pairs a layout with a way of handling machine output and with explicit undo verbs.

| Product | Review layout | How machine output appears | Undo verbs |
|---|---|---|---|
| QuickBooks Online | Dense table with inline editing; Pending, Posted and Excluded lists | Sparkle "Suggested by AI", RULE and Pair badges, a "Ready to post" batch | Accountant edition can undo a whole reconciliation in one step |
| Xero | One card per statement line with Match / Create / Transfer / Discuss tabs; cash coding for up to 200 lines at once | JAX auto-reconciles, labels the method, and lists results on a Reconciled page | **Unreconcile** keeps both records; **Remove & Redo** deletes the created entry |
| Zoho Books | List with a right-hand matches pane and a Multi-select & Match toggle | Rules turn lines into "Recognized" transactions | **Unmatch** versus **Uncategorize**; reconciliations are undone latest first |
| FreeAgent | Explanation form per line | Guessed lines go to For Approval in orange | Bulk approve; Find & Fix history |
| Odoo 19 | List rows with inline suggestion buttons | Reconciliation models, optionally set to auto-validate | Matched counterparts shown in place of the buttons |
| FreshBooks | Two columns: bank lines and FreshBooks entries | "Likely Matches", pre-checked | Unmatch Transactions |

Sources: [QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/matching-rules/learn-updates-new-ai-powered-banking-page/L0hR7A9Zf_ROW_en); [Fit Small Business](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/connect-and-reconcile-bank-account-xero/); [Insightful Accountant](https://insightfulaccountant.com/accounting-tech/general-ledger/10-reasons-why-you-should-be-using-xero-cash-coding); [SaasAnt](https://www.saasant.com/blog/xero-unreconcile-remove-redo/); [Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/help/banking/matching-transactions.md); [Odoo 19](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting/bank/reconciliation.html); [FreshBooks](https://support.freshbooks.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039682172).

The pairs of undo verbs matter more than the layouts. **Unlinking a match is a different operation from deleting the entry the match created**, and both Xero and Zoho expose each separately.

Statement reconciliation is a separate procedure, and nearly every tool runs it the same way: the user enters a statement date and ending balance, the opening balance is carried from the last reconciliation and cannot be edited, a cleared total updates as lines are ticked, and the session can finish only when the **Difference reaches zero** (or be saved for later) ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/statement-reconciliation/reconcile-account-quickbooks-online/L3XzsllsK_ROW_en); [GnuCash](https://code.gnucash.org/docs/C/gnucash-guide/txns-reconcile1.html)). Zoho blocks edits to an account's opening balance once any reconciliation exists, and undoes reconciliations newest first ([Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/help/banking/reconciliation.md)). Several tools offer an explicit way out when a difference won't clear: GnuCash's Balance button posts the remainder to an Orphan account ([GnuCash](https://code.gnucash.org/docs/C/gnucash-guide/txns-reconcile1.html)), Firefly III can store an automated "Reconciliation" correction transaction ([Firefly III](https://docs.firefly-iii.org/how-to/firefly-iii/finances/reconcile/)), and Actual creates a reconciliation transaction before locking the reconciled rows ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/accounts/reconciliation)). Manager.io keeps each reconciliation as a record with a Discrepancy column; clicking a non-zero discrepancy lists the transactions causing it ([Manager.io](https://www.manager.io/guides/bank-reconciliations)). Plain-text tools reach the same result through balance assertions plus cleared and pending flags ([hledger](https://hledger.org/hledger.html)).

The right model for Ledgerflow combines both traditions. Each posting moves through four states (unmarked, pending, cleared, reconciled), each statement session becomes a record holding the statement balance and the difference, and finishing a session writes a balance assertion at the statement date so that later edits breaking reconciled history surface as integrity errors. V1's single `is_reconciled` boolean can express none of this: it has no statement, no balance, no session and no protection.

### Rules are ordered, previewable, and created at the moment of correction

Every surveyed product pairs deterministic rules with learned suggestions and badges the two differently. The rule editors differ mainly in limits and operators:

| Tool | Notable rule features |
|---|---|
| QuickBooks | Up to five conditions on description, raw bank text or amount; All/Any logic; drag-to-reorder priority where the first match wins; an opt-in "auto-add" that skips review ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/banking/how-to-set-and-use-banking-rules-for-downloaded-transactions/00/262188)) |
| Xero | Splits by fixed amount or percentage; rules only propose, and the user still has to press OK ([Insightful Accountant](https://insightfulaccountant.com/accounting-tech/general-ledger/xero-in-on-xero-bank-rules-tips-tricks/)) |
| Zoho Books | The fullest operator set (is, contains, starts with, is empty, numeric comparisons); offers to create a rule after a manual categorization ([Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/help/banking/transaction-rule.md); [Zoho KB](https://www.zoho.com/books/kb/banking/auto-categorization-of-transactions.html)) |
| Sage | Case-sensitive conditions; capped at **300 rules per bank account** ([Sage KB](https://gb-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/view2.jsp?k2dockey=222001000100746)) |
| Odoo | Three model types (one-click button, suggestion, invoice match); regex conditions; payment tolerance; an auto-validate flag; an explicit sequence where "sequencing matters" ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/bank/reconciliation_models.html)) |

The open-source and personal-finance apps contribute the ergonomics. Actual runs rules in pre, default and post stages and ranks them automatically from least to most specific; renaming a payee offers a clean-up rule, and categorizing a transaction creates or updates a rule from that payee's most-used category ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/budgeting/rules/)). Lunch Money's "Create & Apply" previews the matching past transactions and lets the user leave some out, and its "one-time rules" delete themselves after use, for example to retire a manual placeholder once the real import arrives ([Lunch Money](https://support.lunchmoney.app/setup/rules.md)). Firefly III shows what to avoid: its rules cannot mix AND with OR, and their failures are silent ([Firefly III](https://docs.firefly-iii.org/how-to/firefly-iii/features/rules/)). hledger's compact CSV `if` tables offer a rule format an agent can read and write easily ([hledger](https://hledger.org/hledger.html)). Copilot goes the other way: it gives each user their own model, trained by reviewing transactions, and presents that model as a replacement for writing rules by hand ([Copilot](https://intercom.help/copilotmoney/en/articles/8182433-copilot-intelligence-for-spending); [tooldirectory.ai](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/copilot-money)).

Ledgerflow should resolve every incoming line through one precedence chain: an exact match to an open item first, then user rules in explicit priority order, then remembered payee-to-account decisions, then a model suggestion, and only then the Uncategorized account. Ramp's principle should be a hard rule: AI never overwrites a value set by the user or by a rule. Every rule change, including one the agent proposes, should get a dry run that lists the lines it would change.

### Invoices, bills and receipts share one draft, post and allocate lifecycle

The invoice editor looks the same in every product. A header (customer, number, dates, terms) sits above a line grid with per-line tax, followed by totals, notes and terms, and then save variants: Draft, Save and Send, Save and Send Later. Zoho publishes the clearest status model: **Draft, Pending Approval, Sent (emailed or marked sent), Viewed, Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue, Void**. Its record actions include Write Off to bad debt and a reversible Void with a reason ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/books/help/invoice/); [Zoho KB](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/kb/invoices/quotes-invoices-status.md)). A live preview beside the editor is now standard, since both Xero and QuickBooks forced migrations to editors that include one ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/campaign/new-invoicing); [SaasAnt](https://www.saasant.com/blog/edit-invoice-template-quickbooks/)).

The accounting behind the editor is the part V1 is missing. Payments are separate transactions allocated across one or more invoices, credit notes are negative documents with their own number series, and write-offs post to Bad Debt Expense under the direct method ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/bad-debt-recovery-journal-entry)). Aging groups open balances into current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days ([Metabase](https://www.metabase.com/metrics/accounts-receivable-aging)). Cash-basis reports prorate each payment across the invoice's lines: in QuickBooks' example, a $1,000 taxable plus $100 non-taxable invoice paid $200 recognizes 18.18% of it, so $181.80 of taxable sales and $14.54 of tax at 8% ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/sales-taxes/handle-cash-basis-sales-tax/00/248797)).

Collections remain the least developed agent task. Wave sends fixed reminders 3, 7 and 14 days after the due date ([Wave](https://waveapps.com/pro)). Odoo lets the user configure follow-up levels, each with days overdue and a channel such as email, SMS, letter or a manual call task ([Cybrosys](https://www.cybrosys.com/blog/overview-of-follow-up-levels-and-follow-up-in-odoo-18)). QuickBooks' Payments Agent drafts reminders and claims invoices are paid "5 days faster" ([Intuit](https://investors.intuit.com/_assets/_08ee5483ec4c057568cc8774f3fd6aad/intuit/news/2025-07-01_Intuit_Introduces_Ground_Breaking_Virtual_Team_of__1258.pdf)). For service businesses, FreshBooks' "Review Unbilled Time" dialog is the template to copy: date range, projects, and a choice of Detailed, Grouped or Simple lines, with entries marked billed automatically ([FreshBooks, search excerpt](https://support.freshbooks.com/hc/en-us/articles/227602308)).

Bills follow one capture pipeline everywhere: a document is dropped in, forwarded by email or photographed; extraction creates a **draft**; the draft waits in a counted queue and posts only after approval; and it is later matched to the bank payment. The leaders differ in the safeguards around that pipeline. Xero flags duplicate bills automatically, and its JAX Bill Protection inspects "every bill before payment, flagging unusual amounts, altered bank details, or unfamiliar suppliers" ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/accounting-software/pay-bills/); [Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/media-releases/xero-announces-new-ai-innovations-xerocon-london/)). NetSuite Bill Capture shows the source file in a **split view** beside the bill it created ([RSM](https://technologyblog.rsmus.com/uncategorized/netsuite-bill-capture-enhancements-2025-1/)), and Odoo highlights extracted values on the document preview with a "Reload AI data" action ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/vendor_bills/invoice_digitization.html)). Odoo 19 adds red ("highly likely") and yellow ("potential") duplicate warnings and a per-line "Professional percentage" for costs that are partly personal ([Odoo 19 release notes](https://www.odoo.com/odoo-19-release-notes)). FreeAgent matches a receipt to a card line dated within the receipt date plus 2 business days and, if nothing matches, retries every 24 hours for 7 days ([FreeAgent support, search excerpt](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/12583752961554)). Monarch goes furthest: it scans a receipt, finds the matching charge, splits and recategorizes it, writes the details into notes and attaches the image ([Monarch](https://www.monarch.com/blog/winter-release)). Mixed personal and business spending is where many Ledgerflow users live, so that last flow is the exact agent task to copy.

### Tax, currency and reports reward an exact data model

Money storage is not a style choice. A binary float stores $2.78 as 2.7799999713897705078125. In one worked example, computing tax separately rather than inline produced $16.77 against $16.78 ([Modern Treasury](https://www.moderntreasury.com/journal/floats-dont-work-for-storing-cents)). The standard fix is to store a 64-bit integer in the currency's minor unit, alongside its ISO 4217 code ([Modern Treasury](https://www.moderntreasury.com/journal/floats-dont-work-for-storing-cents)). The number of decimals then comes from the code: 0 for JPY, 2 for USD, EUR and GBP, and 3 for BHD, KWD and OMR ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_4217)). Actual's fixed ×100 multiplier, applied even to yen, shows the shortcut to avoid ([Actual](https://actualbudget.org/docs/api/reference)). Rounding is a policy that changes totals. HMRC, for example, lets the total VAT payable on an invoice be rounded down to a whole penny, but treats line-level rounding differently ([HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-trader-records/vatrec12010)). Each document should therefore record its rounding method and store the tax it computed.

Tax is best modelled as codes, not rates. Odoo's model is the most reusable. Each tax has a computation type, an "included in price" flag, a tax group for the invoice totals, and distribution lines that carry **tax grids**. The tax return is then simply the sum of tagged base and tax amounts for a period ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/taxes.html)). A **tax-return lock date** freezes a period once it has been filed ([Odoo forum](https://odoo.com/ko_KR/forum/odoo-for-accounting-firms-35/lock-dates-wizard-266980)). Inclusive prices need the VAT fraction: at 20%, the VAT inside a price is 20/120, or one-sixth ([HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-guide-notice-700)). Reverse charge is a known trap: the Box 4 input tax "is always the same as the Box 1 entry" is "a common myth" ([Tax Adviser](https://www.taxadvisermagazine.com/article/avoiding-trip)). Two features make computed tax explainable, which makes them natural agent tasks: QuickBooks' "See the math" breaks down how the tax on an invoice was calculated ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/sales-tax/set-up-sales-tax/00/185792)), and FreeAgent's VAT Checker flags rates that differ from the default and VAT charged to non-UK contacts before the user files ([FreeAgent support, search excerpt](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35615496100498-Use-FreeAgent-s-VAT-Checker)). UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax began on 6 April 2026 ([FreeAgent](https://freeagent.com/features/mtd-for-income-tax)); an offline app can prepare the figures but cannot file them itself.

Multi-currency follows IAS 21: monetary items are retranslated at the closing rate, and both settlement and revaluation differences go to profit or loss ([LearnSignal](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates/)). In practice every posting stores both its transaction-currency and home-currency amounts, a realized gain or loss posts when the item is settled, and an unrealized revaluation runs at period end and reverses automatically on the first day of the next. Products vary on cadence and visibility. FreeAgent revalues unpaid invoices **daily** and shows a gains panel at the bottom of each invoice ([FreeAgent support, search excerpt](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115001219150)). Zoho requires a note on every manual currency adjustment ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/books/help/accountant/base-currency.html)). NetSuite runs revaluation as a step in its close checklist ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1455781.html)). Frappe Books freezes the exchange rate when a document is submitted ([Frappe Books](https://docs.frappe.io/books/multi-currency-invoicing)).

Reporting quality comes down to a handful of features that the leaders share. Zoho's custom-report builder offers a "Compare With" period comparison, a cash or accrual basis, column choice and a **Classic / Compact / Super Compact** table density ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/books/help/reports/custom-reports.md)). Fava's filter accepts period expressions such as `FY2025-Q3` or `year-1 - year` and handles date filtering correctly, rolling income and expenses before the period into previous earnings while balance-sheet accounts carry their balances forward ([Fava](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/src/fava/help/filters.md)). In Manager.io every total can be clicked open to the transactions behind it ([Manager.io](https://www.manager.io/guides/advanced-queries)). The indirect cash-flow statement starts from net income, adjusts for non-cash items and working capital, and must agree with the change in cash ([AccountingCoach](https://www.accountingcoach.com/cash-flow-statement/explanation)). The most legible budget display is Odoo's, a budget column beside the P&L balance with % variance ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/reporting/budget.html)).

### Close is a padlocked checklist, and the audit trail records field-level diffs

The surveyed products split the close into locks and a checklist. Lock designs vary in how granular they are:

| Product | Lock design |
|---|---|
| Odoo 18 | Separate sales, purchase, tax-return and "lock everything" dates, plus a permanent **hard lock**; exceptions go to named users for a set time with a reason ([Odoo forum](https://odoo.com/ko_KR/forum/odoo-for-accounting-firms-35/lock-dates-wizard-266980); [Cybrosys](https://www.cybrosys.com/odoo/odoo-books/v18/accounting/lock-dates/)) |
| Xero | Two dates: one for everyone except advisers, one for all users ([Chargebee](https://www.chargebee.com/docs/billing/1.0/kb/accounting/xero-lock-dates)) |
| Zoho | Per-module locks that need a reason, with time-boxed partial unlocks ([Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/help/accountant/transaction-lock.md)) |
| QuickBooks | One closing date, plus an **Exceptions to Closing Date** report of changes made after close ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/customer-company-settings/edit-closed-books/L76xHuaZ5_US_en_US)) |
| FreeAgent | Locks automatically once a return is filed or accounts are signed ([FreeAgent support, search excerpt](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115001222324)) |

The checklist side is just as clear. Odoo's year-end list requires banks reconciled, no draft invoices and **suspense accounts closed** before the lock-everything date is set ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/accounting/reporting/year_end.html)). NetSuite's checklist uses status icons and padlocked tasks whose hover text names the prerequisites, and a Modified By column records who changed each status ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1455781.html)).

The audit trails differ in one detail that matters: **whether they record what changed or only that something changed**.

| Product | What the audit trail shows |
|---|---|
| NetSuite System Notes | Type (Create, Set, Change, Unset), field, and old and new values; cannot be edited "by any user, script or app" ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_158644279544.html)) |
| QuickBooks | A global log filterable by user, date and event, kept for **2 years** ([QBO help, search summary](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/audit-log/use-audit-log-quickbooks-online/L2WoVnW6I_US_en_US)) |
| Xero | Only "when a user edited a transaction, but not what they edited", per users ([Xero Product Ideas](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/939198/suggestions/44960347?page=3)) |
| Manager.io | A per-change View button showing the exact values, and import reversal from the same log ([Manager.io](https://www.manager.io/guides/history)) |

The immutability side is settled practice. Once a ledger transaction is posted, "its status and entries are immutable", and undoing it takes "a second reversing Ledger Transaction" ([Modern Treasury](https://docs.moderntreasury.com/reference/ledger-transaction-object)). Mutating in place destroys the evidence of "what changed" ([Modern Treasury](https://moderntreasury.com/journal/enforcing-immutability-in-your-double-entry-ledger)). Fowler names the alternatives: reversal, replacement and difference adjustments ([Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/AccountingNarrative.html)).

## Agents act as proposers under a policy, and the best products make every action explainable and reversible

### Autonomy ranges from "never without approval" to "no human at all"

| Product | Default posture | What decides auto-posting | Where the human reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| QuickBooks agents | Agents "never post transactions or take action without your approval" | A user-editable posting policy: required fields, an amount ceiling, a history window and free-text house rules | A "Ready to post" batch with green, blue or orange confidence icons and a per-transaction **Policy reviews** log |
| Xero JAX | Auto-reconciles high-confidence lines; **off by default**, switched on per bank account | Confidence plus the match method | A Reconciled page listing the method used, with edit or undo |
| Digits | Auto-posts high-confidence items (95%+ claimed) | Confidence and materiality thresholds | A Checklist → Book queue; Confirm stays disabled until a category is set |
| Campfire | Thresholds "adjustable as comfort grows" | User-set confidence thresholds | The close checklist, plus delivery to Slack or email |
| Ramp | Low-risk items such as a "$17 Uber ride" auto-sync | Permissions per capability: code, review, sync | A review list with "Suggested action" badges |
| FreeAgent | Machine-explained lines wait | Fixed Guess precedence | A For Approval tab in orange, with bulk approve |
| Pilot | "Without human involvement" | Undisclosed | An audit history afterwards |

Sources: [QuickBooks Business Feed](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/sg/r/product-update/quickbooks-business-feed/); [QBO Ready to post](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-ca/help-article/account-management/use-ready-post-quickbooks-online/L9jzjf8Xe_CA_en_CA); [Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/xero-rolling-out-jax-powered-automatic-bank-reconciliation); [Digits help](https://help.digits.com/firms-agentic-general-ledger/digits-ai); [Campfire](https://campfire.ai/ember); [Campfire blog](https://campfire.ai/blog/introducing-ember-agents); [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/accounting-agent-launch); [Ramp support, search excerpt](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/45051740591251-Ramp-Accounting-Agent-Enablement-Daily-Use-Admin-guide); [FreeAgent support](https://support.freeagent.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115001586004); [Pilot](https://pilot.com/blog/pilot-unveils-ai-accountant-a-major-leap-toward-artificial-general-intelligence-in-accounting).

The vendors' own statements are sometimes inconsistent. Campfire's pages say both that thresholds decide what auto-applies and that "all agent actions require review before posting" ([Campfire](https://campfire.ai/blog/introducing-ember-agents)). Puzzle promises that "nothing posts to the GL without your explicit sign-off" on a page under a `/wip/` path, while the same page says 85–90% of transactions are categorized automatically by the second or third month ([Puzzle](https://puzzle.io/wip/features/puzzle-ai)). The defensible model for a single-user local app is the conservative one: **propose by default**, with a written policy that lets individual rules or operations post automatically below an amount ceiling. QuickBooks' posting policy is the best configuration surface for this. Its free-text "Transaction posting notes" field is exactly the house-rules document an LLM agent should read before it acts.

### Provenance, evidence and three-tier confidence replace raw scores

No surveyed product shows users a numeric confidence such as "87%". QuickBooks uses three icons: a green check for strong historical support, a blue info icon for some variation among similar past transactions, and an orange alert for limited data ([QBO help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-ca/help-article/account-management/use-ready-post-quickbooks-online/L9jzjf8Xe_CA_en_CA)). Xero instead labels each automated match with the **method** that produced it ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/accounting-software/reconcile-bank-transactions/); [Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/xero-rolling-out-jax-powered-automatic-bank-reconciliation)):

| Method | What it means |
|---|---|
| Rule | One of the user's bank rules was applied |
| Match | JAX found an existing document |
| Memory | Based on how the user reconciled similar past transactions |
| Prediction | Based on how all Xero users reconciled similar transactions |
| Manual | Done by hand |

Other products show the evidence behind a suggestion. Booke shows the "Reasoning Behind the AI's Suggestion" and the "Influential Historical Transactions" ([Booke](https://booke.ai/xero)). QuickBooks lists the past transactions it considered similar ([QBO help, search summary](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-ca/help-article/account-management/use-ready-post-quickbooks-online/L9jzjf8Xe_CA_en_CA)).

For Ledgerflow, every agent-made posting should carry its method, a three-level confidence, a one-line reason, and links to its evidence (the statement line, the document, the rule ID and up to five similar past transactions), all shown in the inspector. The point is not decoration: when a user can see why an entry was made, a correction becomes a teaching act instead of a guess.

### Corrections should become visible rules, not silent model drift

There are two camps on learning. One learns in the background: Digits does not "use fixed if-this-then-that rules" ([Digits help](https://help.digits.com/firms-agentic-general-ledger/digits-ai)), and Copilot's per-user model is described as "eliminating the need for manual rule creation" ([tooldirectory.ai](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/copilot-money)). The other turns corrections into explicit logic. Ramp converts a plain-language correction such as "Code Uber rides for the LA sales team to Client travel going forward" into a standing instruction ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/accounting-agent-launch)), and Puzzle's draft close logic passes through Describe, Lock and Approve so that it becomes deterministic: "Same inputs, same outputs. Every run" ([Puzzle](https://puzzle.io/wip/features/puzzle-ai)). For an app run by a large language model, the second camp is clearly right. The model itself is not deterministic, so its memory should be something the user can see, replay and test. The agent should **write rules** and the rule engine should apply them, leaving the model only the long tail of first-time vendors. This also answers the practitioners' strongest objection: errors are **systematic**, so one vendor coded wrong every time slips past spot checks ([beancount.io forum, low reliability](https://beancount.io/forum/t/the-80-20-reality-ai-categorizes-80-but-i-still-review-100-of-transactions/3217)). Grouping the review queue by payee and by rule lets one decision fix every line from a vendor and then lock that decision into a rule.

### One inbox, a feed of agent work, and answers with sources

QuickBooks' Business Feed collects agent output as tiles and describes itself as "the finish line for work that needs your human review", with an empty feed as the normal state ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/sg/r/product-update/quickbooks-business-feed/)). Questions back to the human are part of the same loop: Puzzle emails uncategorized lines to the client and categorizes from the natural-language reply ([Puzzle](https://puzzle.io/blog/puzzle-accounting-ai)), QuickBooks turns comments on bank lines into a request list ([Porte Brown](https://blog.portebrown.com/quickbooks-onlines-new-bank-feed-what-to-expect-in-october-2025)), and Xero keeps a Discuss tab on unresolved lines ([Fit Small Business](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/connect-and-reconcile-bank-account-xero/)). In Ledgerflow the same role falls to a "needs info" state on a proposal, answered in the Agent tab.

Natural-language Q&A has become standard, and what separates the good implementations is **source attribution**. Campfire's answers to "Why did costs spike in Q3?" link to GL accounts, transactions and documents ([Campfire](https://campfire.ai/ember)). Rillet's flux explanations trace a variance to vendors and timing, keep their source links in exports, and accept "book a journal entry for me" as a request ([Rillet](https://www.rillet.com/blog/january-2026-product-update)). Zoho's CoCreate shows the agent filling a form in real time and lets the human take over partway through ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/ai-features/ai-features.html)). This is the right model for agent-created invoices in Ledgerflow: the user watches the draft form fill in the main pane, not in a chat bubble.

### MCP turned ledgers into agent tool surfaces, and open-source servers show the safety pattern

Commercial vendors now expose their ledgers through MCP. NetSuite's AI Connector Service lets organizations choose models, "define the data they can access", and run standard or custom tools under role permissions ([ITPro](https://itpro.com/software/netsuite-announces-new-mcp-apps-for-ai-connector-service-allowing-users-to-access-financial-data-directly-within-claude)). Xero reports that use of its MCP server grew **10x** between December 2025 and May 2026 ([Xero](https://www.xero.com/media-releases/new-ai-innovations-xerocon-denver/)), and Rillet gives Claude read access under its own role-based permissions ([Rillet](https://www.rillet.com/blog/mcp-aura-flow-and-aura-agents-which-one-for-which-job)). The open-source servers are more instructive, because they publish their safety design:

| Server | Safety pattern |
|---|---|
| gnucash-mcp (86 tools) | One "orientation" dashboard call. A statement "rehearsal" dry run sorts each line into new, already entered, or needs a look. Commit "refuses wholesale rather than land a month that doesn't tie". Backups are taken before the first write of a session. Reconciled splits are protected, and voiding is separate from deleting ([gnucash-mcp](https://github.com/ninetails-io/gnucash-mcp)) |
| hledger-mcp | A `dryRun` option on every write tool, validation with `hledger check`, and an optional read-only mode ([hledger-mcp](https://github.com/iiAtlas/hledger-mcp)) |
| actual-mcp | Amounts returned as integer cents with an `amountsIn` sign-convention field, and writes disabled unless explicitly enabled ([actual-mcp](https://github.com/s-stefanov/actual-mcp)) |
| Open Accounting Bench | Grades agents on the ledger state they leave behind, where "a balanced-but-misclassified entry fails" ([Open Accounting Bench](https://github.com/bex-co/open-accounting-bench)) |

Ledgerflow's operation layer should adopt the whole pattern: orientation, query, propose with a dry run, a commit that re-validates and refuses atomically, a snapshot per session, protection for reconciled and locked postings, and void instead of delete. Because the agent and the UI share one operation surface, the same guarantees then cover human clicks as well.

### The failure modes are systematic errors, unverifiable accuracy and forced rollouts

Accuracy claims rarely survive scrutiny. Digits' widely quoted benchmark, 97.8% accuracy for its agent against 79.1% for 12 outsourced accountants on 2,000 transactions, was published at its June 2025 launch ([Insightful Accountant](https://blog.insightfulaccountant.com/digits-launches-ai-agents-for-its-autonomous-general-ledger)). Its help centre later calls the same figures "independent 2026 testing" ([Digits help](https://help.digits.com/firms-agentic-general-ledger/digits-ai)), so the test is best treated as vendor-sponsored. Pilot and Docyt publish no benchmarks at all.

Intuit itself advises users to "review all outputs carefully" ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/sg/r/product-update/quickbooks-business-feed/)). A competitor's blog reports QuickBooks users "spending time every week fixing miscategorized transactions" after agents rolled out with no opt-out ([usecarly.com](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/quickbooks-ai)).

Learning across customers is a privacy decision as well. Digits learns at client, firm and global level ([Digits help](https://help.digits.com/firms-agentic-general-ledger/digits-ai)). Booke markets the opposite: an isolated model per client ([Booke](https://booke.ai/uk)). For a local app, a client-only model is free by construction. The remaining privacy risk is what the agent sends to its model provider, which beanquery-mcp warns about explicitly ([beanquery-mcp](https://github.com/vanto/beanquery-mcp)).

Because no vendor's accuracy can be verified, Ledgerflow should keep its own scoreboard each month, following Booke's monthly report of AI-handled against human-handled work ([Booke](https://booke.ai/xero)). It should count lines that were proposed, auto-posted, accepted as-is, corrected and reverted.

## Dense tables, keyboard triage and colour reserved for meaning define the design language

### Density regressions cost incumbents more than missing features

The loudest UX failures in this survey were readability regressions, not missing features. QuickBooks' 2025 banking table drew "harder to read" and "adds more resistance to my workflow" complaints ([QB Community](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/community/banking-4/new-banking-page-not-good-24079)). Xero lets users switch between an enlarged view and a compact one "that displays more transactions on screen" ([Xero blog](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/new-look-bank-reconciliation/)). Zoho ships three report densities ([Zoho](https://zstatic.zohostatic.com/books/help/reports/custom-reports.md)). Density without guidance also fails. NetSuite's "dense layouts slow down workflows" ([PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/questions/what-needs-improvement-with-netsuite-erp)), and Mercury's logged-in experience is criticized as "being dropped into a cockpit" ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/mercury-design-breakdown)). The fix is to pair density with in-place guidance, such as Odoo 19's "use this account when…" descriptions, NetSuite's padlock hover text naming the prerequisites, and QuickBooks' "See the math". Those small explanations are what make a flat, compact, high-contrast interface usable rather than merely dense.

### Keyboard maps exist, but nobody documents keyboard review of agent proposals

| Product | Keyboard pattern | Example keys |
|---|---|---|
| Zoho Books | Module jumps, create chords, and a sheet where every shortcut can be edited | Shift+3 Banking, C then I new invoice, Shift+? sheet |
| Fava | Two-key "go to" and filter focus | g i income statement, g j journal, f t time filter, ? overlay |

### Colour carries meaning or it is not used

The products with the best design reputations use colour only for state. Mercury's colour system "is built around calm" and keeps red and yellow for errors and warnings ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/mercury-design-breakdown)). YNAB pairs each status colour with an icon so colour is never the only signal: a calendar for an underfunded upcoming bill, a pie chart for target progress, a check for a target met ([YNAB](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/colors-and-icons-in-your-plan-HJQv_XHko.md)). Fava works from a small set of semantic tokens (error, warning, green), colours only gains and losses, and offers options to invert gain/loss colours and to flip the sign of credit-normal accounts for display ([Fava options](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/src/fava/help/options.md); [Fava CSS](https://github.com/beancount/fava/tree/main/frontend/css)). Frappe Books shows balances with a Dr/Cr suffix instead of colour ([Frappe Books](https://docs.frappe.io/books/chart-of-accounts)).

Machine-made entries get one reserved signal in each product: FreeAgent uses orange, Wave purple, QuickBooks a sparkle, and Copilot a light-blue dot. This matches the user's existing rule of near-monochrome ink with an accent only for AI proposals. One gap in the research: no primary source documents how any product aligns money columns or shows negatives (minus sign, parentheses or red). The recommendation of right-aligned tabular numerals with a leading minus is standard practice, not something this survey found evidence for.

### Mapping the evidence onto a CAD-Workshop-style shell

The user's preferred CAD-Workshop-style layout fits the evidence closely. A narrow icon rail on the left replaces V1's 8-item text sidebar and carries Fava-style `g`+letter shortcuts. The title bar's status chips become the queue-first home page that Odoo's "x to reconcile" journal cards and NetSuite's reminder portlets point toward: Inbox count, accounts with stale reconciliations, overdue invoices, days to the next tax deadline, integrity errors, pending agent proposals and the current lock date, each opening its filtered queue and together replacing V1's six-KPI dashboard ([Odoo 19](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/finance/accounting/bank/reconciliation.html); [Fava help](https://github.com/beancount/fava/blob/main/src/fava/help/features.md)). The main area is hairline-divided panels holding dense tables, with a density toggle. The right-hand inspector carries four tabs: Details for editable fields; Evidence for the source statement line, the document preview with extracted fields highlighted, the matched rule and similar past transactions; History for field-level diffs by actor; and Agent for a chat scoped to the current selection, the agent's pending proposals for it, and the session log with its revert button.

## Ledgerflow V1 lacks foundations, not screens

V1 has a credible feature list for a simple ledger: a seeded chart of accounts, double-entry journal lines, quick income, expense and transfer forms, invoices, bills, four reports and a dashboard. Its quick forms and soft-deleted contacts are worth keeping. The forms match FreshBooks' plain-word canned entries and Manager.io's receipt and payment documents, which both put friendly language over real postings. Measured against 2026 practice, though, the most serious gaps sit below the screens. They also compound: an agent cannot safely operate a ledger that has no audit log, no locks and no reconciliation records.

Three of these gaps deserve emphasis. **Float money is a correctness bug, not a style issue**: any total that passes through tax rounding or a percentage split can drift by a cent, and once it does, a reconciliation that should tie never reaches exactly zero. The **`is_reconciled` boolean merges two different jobs**, clearing a line against a bank record and proving a statement balance, and because neither job leaves a record, nothing stops a reconciled entry from being edited afterwards. Finally, V1 stores **"overdue" as a status rather than deriving it from the due date and the allocations**, and it applies **one global tax rate**. Both fail as soon as a business has partial payments or mixed-rate invoices, such as UK standard, reduced and zero-rated lines on one document. The brief does not say whether V1's 20 seeded accounts include the clearing accounts an import pipeline needs: Uncategorized Income and Expense, Suspense, Transfers in Transit, Opening Balance Equity, VAT control and FX gain/loss. The redesign's seed must include them.

## What the redesign should build: the ledger core first, then seven dense views

### A data model that makes wrong states impossible

The PocketBase schema should mirror the plain-text model and add the document and agent layers on top:

| Group | Collections and key fields |
|---|---|
| Core ledger | **Accounts:** code, path, type, normal side, a `contra_of` link, a cash-flow category, a "use this when" description, currency, and an archived flag. **Currencies:** with their exponent. **Transactions:** date, contact, narration, status (proposed, posted or voided), kind, links, tags, `reverses_id`, and source fingerprint. **Postings:** a signed integer `amount_minor`, currency, `home_amount_minor`, rate, contact, tax code, memo, reconciliation state, and reconciliation and statement-line references |
| Bank side | **Statement imports:** file hash, mapping profile, period, opening and closing balances. **Statement lines:** bank ID, fingerprint, occurrence index, raw row, inbox status. **Reconciliations.** **Balance assertions** |
| Sales and purchases | **Documents:** invoices, bills, credit notes and vendor credits, with lines. **Allocations.** **Files:** content hash and extracted fields |
| Tax and FX | **Tax codes:** with components and report boxes. **Tax returns:** a snapshot of included line IDs. **Rates** |
| Automation and control | **Rules.** **Recurring templates.** **Budgets.** **Locks** |
| Agent and audit | **Proposals:** method, confidence tier, reason, evidence references, and the ledger version read. **Events:** actor, session, operation, entity, before, after, and reason. **Agent sessions:** with their pre-write snapshot |

Two storage rules make this safe. JavaScript numbers hold integers exactly up to 2^53−1, about 9 quadrillion minor units, which is ample for household and small-business books. So integer minor units can live in ordinary number fields, as long as a single command layer in the hooks rejects anything that isn't an integer. Rates and quantities should travel as decimal strings and never pass through `parseFloat`.

The command layer enforces the hard invariants on every write and rejects the write if any fails: postings sum to zero in home currency (and per currency for single-currency transactions), the account is open and can take postings, the date falls after the relevant lock, reconciled or allocated postings are untouched, allocations never exceed the open balance, and bank IDs and document numbers are unique. Soft checks run on demand and before close, Fava-style. They cover likely duplicates, stale reconciliations, non-zero Suspense or Uncategorized balances, AR/AP control accounts that don't tie, statement gaps, and missed recurring runs. They return machine-readable findings that both the human and the agent can act on.

### One operation contract for the human and the agent

Each UI gesture is one named operation, such as `import.file`, `inbox.accept`, `txn.categorize`, `rule.create`, `recon.finish`, `invoice.approve`, `payment.allocate` or `period.lock`. Each operation accepts `dry_run`, which returns the diff, the validation result and the number of records affected; commits atomically or refuses outright; and writes an event recording the actor (user, agent, rule or import) and the session.

Agent writes land as **proposals** by default. A posting policy, modelled on QuickBooks' and combined with Ramp's per-capability permissions, can promote specific operations or rules to auto-post below an amount ceiling. A few actions stay outside the agent's reach altogether, namely moving or removing a lock date, unreconciling, deleting a posted record, and changing the base currency or rounding policy; the agent may propose them, but only a user confirmation applies them. Xero's two-lock model maps directly onto the agent: an **agent lock date** set earlier than the human one lets the human post late adjustments that the agent cannot touch. Before an agent session's first write, the app snapshots the ledger. "Revert session" then voids the proposals and posts reversals for anything already posted.

No source documents what happens when a user edits a record the agent has a pending proposal for. That gap is unaddressed in the research. A version check, in which proposals carry the record version they read and are marked stale if it changes, is a reasonable local policy.

The agent's job list follows Digits' six stages, plus collections:

| Stage | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Collect | Parse files, propose CSV mappings, and ask when dates fit both D/M and M/D |
| Book | Apply rules, then memory, then model, then Uncategorized; propose a rule after repeated confirmations |
| Reconcile | Match lines and pre-fill the statement session from the file's closing balance |
| Schedule | Generate recurring items and accruals that reverse automatically |
| Review | Run integrity checks and flag anomalies, such as a usual $72 bill arriving as $250 |
| Report | Explain any number with a period-over-period breakdown that links to the underlying lines, and pre-fill the close checklist |
| Collections | Draft a reminder for each overdue invoice for the user to send |

Sources: [Digits](https://digits.com/product/agentic-close/); [QuickBooks AU](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/au/blog/accountants-and-bookkeepers/quickbooks-intuit-ai-agents-for-accountants/).

### Seven work views, each a dense table with the inspector

| View | Main pane | Key operations | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inbox | Unreviewed statement lines, unmatched documents and agent proposals, groupable by payee, rule or method | Accept, edit, split, mark transfer, approve and make rule, defer | J/K to move, X to select, Enter to accept, Shift+Enter to accept and make a rule, C account, P payee, T tax code; pre-checked high-confidence batch |
| Ledger | Chart-of-accounts tree on the left; register with running, cleared and uncleared totals | Quick income, expense and transfer forms; journal entry; void; reverse | Fava-style filter bar (time, account, advanced) whose filters the agent can write |

Rules, tax codes, contacts, currencies, the posting policy and preferences belong in a Setup area reached from the rail. They don't need their own work views. This follows the user's standing direction to build only the views the job needs.

The build order follows the dependencies. First come the money and postings core, the event log and operation layer, import with staging, rules, matching, Ledger, Reconcile, the lock date and the agent tab. Everything else depends on these. Next come the sales and purchase documents with allocations, document capture, tax codes and returns, the full report set, recurring templates, the close checklist and plain-text export. Last come multi-currency revaluation, CAMT.053 and MT940, budgets and the cash forecast.

## Conclusion

The surveyed products no longer compete on raw automation rate. They compete on **auditable autonomy**: how clearly each machine action states its method, evidence and confidence, how cheaply it can be undone, and how precisely the user can set what the machine may do alone. Here a local, single-user agent app has a structural advantage. Every vendor surveyed claims a "full audit trail", yet none publicly documents an immutable ledger with replay; incumbents bolt review screens onto mutable cloud ledgers. Ledgerflow can route every human click and every agent call through one validating command layer that dry-runs, records the actor and reverts by session. That is a stronger guarantee than any surveyed product documents, and it costs little in a local app. Three other findings change what "production level" should mean for this redesign. Keyboard review of agent proposals is undocumented anywhere, so it is the most distinctive thing Ledgerflow can ship. The offline constraint removes the most common complaint in the category, broken bank connections, so the effort saved should go into import and de-duplication. And exporting a Beancount or hledger journal lets users verify their books with an independent checker, which answers Bench more convincingly than any promise.

The evidence has clear limits. Every AI accuracy figure here is vendor-reported or vendor-sponsored. Jurisdiction-specific tax rules beyond the UK examples (EU OSS, US state sales tax, GST/HST) were not researched and should be treated as plug-in modules. Several UI details rest on search excerpts of help pages that could not be fetched. No primary source documents money-column alignment or negative-number styling in any product. None of these gaps changes the central recommendation: fix the storage, the operation contract and the audit model before drawing a single new screen, because every view and every agent behaviour above depends on them.
