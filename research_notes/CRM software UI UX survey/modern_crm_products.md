# Modern CRM products: UI, UX and design language (state as of late 2026)

Scope note: researched 2026-10-29. Dates are given wherever the source carries them; anything from before 2025 is marked "(older)". Help-center pages are undated living docs and are treated as current. Several claims come from search-engine summaries of pages I could not fetch in full; those are marked "(snippet)" and should be treated as lower confidence.

## Q0. Product-by-product catalogue (IA, main screens, interactions, visual style, reviewer verdicts)

### Takeaway
The modern leaders (Attio, Folk, Twenty, Close) share one model: a quiet left sidebar of objects, lists and favorites; spreadsheet-grade tables and kanban boards as saved "views" over the same records; a record page made of a header, a highlights strip, tabs and a details sidebar; and Cmd+K/"/" for actions and search. The incumbents (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive) spent 2025-2026 converging on the same layout: HubSpot's two-column record with tabs and a right sidebar, Zoho's vertical left nav, and Salesforce's lighter Cosmos theme.

### Cited Findings

#### Attio (deepest coverage)
- **Sidebar IA**: a control panel (workspace switcher, settings, invites, apps), a search section (`cmd + k` for commands, `/` for full-CRM search), a navigation panel ("Home page, notifications inbox, tasks, notes, emails, reports, sequences, and workflows"), a Records section listing every object, a Lists section ("every list you have created or has been shared with you"), and a Chats section for Ask Attio conversations — [Attio: Navigating Attio](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-101/navigating-attio)
- **Favorites**: you can favorite dashboards, workflows, sequences, notes, templates, records, list views and Ask Attio chats, and group them into folders by drag and drop. "Favorites and folders are personal to you and won't appear in other team members' sidebars." The sidebar shows six lists by default ("Show all lists" removes the limit), and lists can be sorted by Most relevant, Recently added, Alphabetical or Custom — [Attio: Navigating your workspace](https://attio.com/help/reference/tips-and-tricks/quick-actions)
- **Command menu and shortcuts**: `cmd + k` opens quick-action suggestions "which will differ depending on where you are in the platform". It also shows upcoming meetings for today and tomorrow, with RSVP status as colored circles, and can create meeting notes. Search (`/`) finds records "by name, email, domain, phone number, or ID", plus descriptions, social handles and locations. Single-key shortcuts: `n` new note, `t` new task, `c` compose email, `?` help and the full shortcut list. Attio has "over 30 quick actions" — [Attio: Navigating your workspace](https://attio.com/help/reference/tips-and-tricks/quick-actions)
- **Record page anatomy**: a header with the ⋮ menu (Configure page, admin only) and a row of action icons (reorderable). The **Overview** tab carries up to **six "highlight widgets"** at the top. Default tabs are **Overview, Activity, Notes, Tasks, Emails, Files**; "+ Add tab" adds more, and some defaults can't be removed. **Relationship tabs** list linked records with chosen columns, and you can have several **Emails tabs** with different sources. The **sidebar has a mandatory "Record Details" section**, which shows the first five attributes unless configured. Custom attribute sections can be added and reordered, and there is a **Lists section** (which lists the record is in) that can't be removed. Configuration is per object — [Attio: Configure record pages](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/records/configure-record-pages)
- **Table views**: View settings → "+ Add column". Columns reorder by drag, resize by dragging the header edge, and can be relabeled without renaming the attribute. Dates can show as relative ("in 3 days"), and currency symbol and decimals are configurable. Cells are edited inline. **Multi-cell selection by click-drag or shift+click, and copy/paste (cmd/ctrl+C/V) works between Attio and external spreadsheets.** "+ Add calculation" at the bottom of a column adds an aggregate (numeric), or empty/filled counts for other types. Arrow keys move between cells and Enter saves — [Attio: Create and manage table views](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/views/create-and-manage-table-views)
- **Kanban views**: grouped by any **status** attribute. Card attributes are configurable and reorderable, labels can be toggled, and "Hide empty attributes" removes blank rows. Hovering a card reveals "Edit entry" and "Open preview", and the card footer has buttons for note, task and comment. Cards can be **collapsed** to the name only. A collapsed column shows "only the stage color, name, and record count". "+ Add calculation" sits at the bottom of each stage. **"Track time in stage"** takes a target time, and the counter in the card's bottom-right corner turns red once it is exceeded. Several checked cards can be dragged at once. A per-stage **Confetti** toggle fires "a confetti canon" when a card enters that stage. Relationship attributes (e.g. company data on deal cards) can be shown on cards — [Attio: Create and manage kanban views](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/views/create-and-manage-kanban-views)
- **2026 visual-system changes**: "Icons and avatars are now a standard 16px across the product, for a cleaner, more consistent interface." Settings search covers every tab, and Ask Attio gained native dictation (Aug 25, 2026) — [Attio changelog Aug 25 2026](https://attio.com/changelog/2026/changelog-august-25-2026). Ask Attio "reasoning steps are now hidden by default", search works with a full URL, and "Ask Attio can now adjust your app theme and accent colors" on Enterprise (Apr 29, 2026) — [Attio changelog Apr 29 2026](https://attio.com/changelog/2026/changelog-april-29-2026). Pasting an address fills the individual location fields, iOS record and table views "load ten times faster", and Ask Attio shows the credits each thread uses (Sep 23, 2026) — [Attio changelog Sep 23 2026](https://attio.com/changelog/2026/changelog-september-23-2026)
- **AI attributes (generated columns)**: an "AI autofill" toggle on any attribute, with modes Summarize record, Research agent (web research plus record context), Classify record, and Prompt completion with attribute variables. Values regenerate per cell (a recalculate icon on hover) or for a whole column (column header → "Recalculate values with AI") — [Attio: AI attributes](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-ai/ai-attributes) (snippet); [Craftt: Attio AI in 2026](https://www.craftt.io/blog/attio-ai-features)
- **Reviewer verdicts**: praised as a "Notion-like interface", "intuitive, elegant, and modern", with "filtering 20k+ records happens within milliseconds with no lag". Criticised because "Setting up workflows and webhooks is not intuitive", navigation between objects takes "3-4 clicks", and one team "didn't do anything on it for three months because we didn't know how to build lists" — [Hackceleration Attio review 2026](https://www.hackceleration.com/labs/review/attio). G2 reviewers praise ease of use but cite a learning curve and gaps in reporting and email sharing — [G2 Attio reviews](https://g2.com/products/attio/reviews) (snippet)

#### Folk (deep)
- **Data model and IA**: records (people and companies) live workspace-wide. **Groups** are per-workflow databases ("Leads, Investors, Clients, Candidates") and a record can sit in many groups. Fields are native, "smart" (computed by folk) or **custom, scoped to a group**. **Views** are saved table or pipeline (kanban) layouts inside a group — [folk: data model](https://help.folk.app/en/articles/9790806-folk-data-model)
- **Views**: created from "+ New view" in the sidebar by choosing Table or Pipeline. Fields are shown or hidden from a view icon at top right — [Guideflow: create pipeline view in folk](https://www.guideflow.com/tutorial/how-to-create-pipeline-view-in-folk). Table views can be published as a read-only public link, but "pipeline views can't be shared yet". Group permissions are Owner, Full access, Can edit and Can view — [folk: Share a view](https://help.folk.app/en/articles/6505030-share-a-view)
- **Timeline of UI releases**: "Side peek" (open a profile on the right while browsing on the left), Jan 9 2024 (older). Pipeline keyboard shortcuts, Feb 22 2024 (older). Deals as objects, Nov 14 2024 (older). Deals table views, Mar 14 2025. "Sidebar summer glow-up" ("smart preview of groups and quick actions"), Jul 22 2025. AI Follow-ups, Jan 9 2025. Recap Assistant, Dec 17 2025. Assistants library, Jan 15 2026. **AI Fields** ("Fields that autonomously update your CRM"), Mar 17 2026. "Auto-fill AI", Apr 29 2026. Dashboards leveled up, Dec 11 2025. ChatGPT integration, Sep 11 2025. WhatsApp, Jun 26 2025. "Privacy on your terms" (hide sensitive interactions), Jan 20 2026. Mobile app "Contacts by folk", Jul 16 2026 — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- **Pipeline keyboard model**: arrow keys move around the board, Mod+A selects all, Escape selects none, Alt+X or Mod+click toggles a card, and selection key + arrows or click selects a range — [folk changelog: keyboard shortcuts on pipeline](https://www.folk.app/zh/changelog/keyboard-shortcuts-on-pipeline) (snippet; 2024, older)
- **AI assistants (Oct 2025 onward)**: Follow-up (flags stalled email and WhatsApp threads), Recap (summarises the relationship across email, meetings, notes and LinkedIn), Research (Perplexity-powered), and Workflow (triggers personalised emails). "Magic fields" are "AI-generated columns computed from contact data" — [Dupple folk review, Jul 2026](https://dupple.com/reviews/folk)
- **Capture**: the folkX extension pulls profiles from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, X, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok "in one click", described as "the core competence, not a bolt-on" — [Dupple folk review](https://dupple.com/reviews/folk)
- **Reviewer verdicts**: "Beautiful UX" that "feels like Notion, not Salesforce", but mobile is "less mature" — [Dupple folk review](https://dupple.com/reviews/folk). One comparison found Folk the "more seamless UI" that "feels more like Airtable or Notion" while "Attio is just a bit slower", and noted that Folk keeps tasks, notes and reports "more hidden behind the core spreadsheet view" — [Whalesync: Attio vs Folk](https://www.whalesync.com/blog/attio-vs-folk). Folk's automation is described as minimal and its integrations as thin — [Rework: Folk alternatives](https://resources.rework.com/vi/tools/crm/best-folk-alternatives) (snippet)

#### HubSpot Smart CRM (deep; 2026 refresh)
- **2026 visual refresh**: opt-in from **June 30, 2026** and the mandatory default from **August 31, 2026**. "Lighter typography, more intentional spacing, and less reliance on heavy headers so record and list content takes center stage." "The navigation structure isn't changing." AI-first changes follow in stages over the next six months, and mobile gets a "unified Breeze and search input" — [Vantagepoint: HubSpot 2026 redesign](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/hs/hubspot-2026-redesign-what-to-know)
- **Index page (list) redesign**: public beta **May 27, 2026**, mandatory "late July 2026". It moves view settings onto the index, adds "collapsible filters and summary metrics", shows data as a report on the index itself, adds "Breeze AI column insights", and puts adding columns, creating properties and Data Agent "smart property" creation behind a single button. Smart properties "auto-fill an AI-generated property across records", and the advice is to pilot on a sample first — [Vantagepoint: Smart CRM index redesign](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/hs/hubspot-smart-crm-index-redesign-guide)
- **Updated default record layout** (two-column; Universal record page beta; customised portals must reset from end of March 2026). **Header**: up to five chosen properties, an editable avatar, two quick activity buttons, prev/next pagination arrows, breadcrumbs, a record-type tag, and an Actions menu (customize, property and association history, AI summary, merge, clone, delete). **Tabs**: Overview (collapsible Summary with AI insights, plus Health with sentiment), Activities (timeline filterable by date, owner, properties and type), Revenue, and Intelligence (enrichment). **Right sidebar**: a Key information card with inline editing, Associations (switchable between "List" and "Chips"), and Attachments (with a "Focused" filter for "the most relevant attachments only"). Admins get an edit mode with custom tabs and per-team views — [HubSpot KB: default record layout](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/understand-the-default-record-layout)
- **Board view**: properties on cards are edited inline (hover → edit icon → pop-up). Cmd/Ctrl+click multi-selects cards, "select all in pipeline" is available, and bulk edit works through a dialog. Cards sort by property or by manual vertical drag within a lane, and note and task icons sit on each card — [HubSpot KB: board view](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/manage-records-in-board-view). Columns collapse with a caret icon — [HubSpot KB (snippet)](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/manage-records-in-board-view?b_id=1524)
- **Breeze record summary card**: on the record page and in the hover **Preview** side panel. It "generates immediately on preview or record page load", can be refreshed, and shows a table of properties followed by paragraphs summarising notes and activities — [HubSpot KB: summarize records](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/summarize-records)
- **Keyboard support is weak**: native CRM shortcuts are not documented, and users request hotkeys or install third-party Chrome extensions (e.g. D for Deals, N for create) — [HubSpot community idea](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Program-hot-keys-for-quick-navigation/idc-p/460490) (snippet)
- **Reviewer complaints (older, 2021)**: "UI looks fifteen years old", "clunky" to navigate tabs and log calls, settings hidden — [RevOps Co-op 2021 CRM review](https://www.revopscoop.com/post/the-2021-rev-ops-co-op-crm-product-review) (snippet; older). Reddit users describe HubSpot as "bloated" — [Dupple: best CRM for startups 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/best-crm-for-startups) (snippet)

#### Pipedrive (deep)
- **Pipeline (kanban)**: cards show "title, contact, value, label and owner" plus an activity icon for upcoming activities. Cards drag between stages, and **drop zones at the bottom** mark a deal won or lost or delete it; closed deals leave the board. Filters, sorting (default: next activity) and a pipeline switcher dropdown are on the board. A **"Rotting"** feature highlights deals not updated recently. Card fields and layout are customizable: "how stages are displayed, how deal detail views open and whether pinned or quick filters are visible" — [Pipedrive KB: pipeline view](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/pipeline-view)
- **Deal detail page**: a **stage progress bar** shows the current stage and "the number of days it took to complete each stage". The **left sidebar Summary** holds deal score, value and products, revenue metrics, label, probability, expected close date, contact person and organization; it is customizable and changes apply company-wide. The main area has composer tabs (notes, activity, email, files, documents). A **Focus** section collects "activities, email drafts and pinned notes that require your attention", above a **History** of everything plus field changes, and there is a Changelog tab. Hover cards appear on people and orgs — [Pipedrive KB: deal detail view](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/deal-detail-view)
- **Detail-view redesign reaction (2023-24, older)**: the new detail view was left-aligned so it can sit beside other tools. Users complained that on large screens "all information is concentrated on the left side leaving half the window empty" — [Pipedrive community: New details screen for Deals](https://community.pipedrive.com/discussion/12010/new-details-screen-for-deals) (snippet)
- **Navigation (older, 2020 redesign, still the base)**: a left sidebar with Leads Inbox and Deals at the top, then email, contacts, calendar and Insights, with the rest under "More". Search sits top-center with a quick-add (+) beside it — [Pipedrive blog: navigation announcement](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/blog/pipedrive-navigation-announcement). An Interface preferences page lets users drag-arrange sidebar items, pick a landing page and switch keyboard shortcuts on or off — [Pipedrive: interface preferences](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/crm/features/interface-preferences) (snippet)
- **2026**: a "Project tasks" side panel inside the Activities list view and a public MCP server for ChatGPT and Claude (June 2026) — [Pipedrive product updates](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/product-updates) (snippet). **Nova** (Sep 16, 2026), an AI meeting assistant that "proposes CRM updates for review" — [LetsDataScience: Pipedrive Nova](https://letsdatascience.com/news/pipedrive-launches-nova-ai-assistant-for-crm-updates-52a0540c) (snippet)

#### Salesforce Lightning (deep)
- **Cosmos theme on SLDS 2**: "Rounded corners in the new UI indicate that an element is interactive. Elements in the interface with square corners aren't interactive." Icons are more legible at all scales, the type scale is improved, colors are "cleaner, brighter, higher-contrast", buttons use "drop shadows and gradients", and spacing is "adaptable". It is enabled via Setup > Themes and Branding. Trailhead dates SLDS 2 availability across orgs to Feb 21, 2025 — [Trailhead: Discover Enhanced Lightning UI](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/enhanced-lightning-ui-quick-look/discover-enhanced-lightning-ui)
- **Record page anatomy (classic Lightning, still current)**: a **highlights panel** at the top is driven by the compact layout, which holds up to 10 fields with the first 7 displayed. The same compact layout feeds hover lookup cards and expanded activity-timeline items. Tabs (Details, Activity, Chatter, Related) and the Path component sit below — [Salesforce docs: compact layouts](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.210.0.salesforce1appadmin.meta/salesforce1appadmin/s1_admin_guide_compact_layouts_intro.htm) (older doc, concept unchanged). **Dynamic Forms** put individual fields and sections on the page as components with visibility rules (by profile, device or field value) — [Atrium: Dynamic Forms](https://atrium.ai/resources/salesforce-dynamic-forms-beyond-drag-and-drop-design/)
- **Kanban**: grouped by Stage, with "Summarize By" Amount shown at the top of each column and recalculated live on drag. Card fields are chosen in "Select Fields to Display" — [Trailhead: opportunities in Kanban](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/projects/customize-a-sales-path-for-your-team/work-with-opportunities-in-the-kanban-view)
- **2026**: Sales Cloud is rebranded "Agentforce Sales". Spring '26 adds a "Request Approval" record-page component and lets screen flows run in full Lightning with the Cosmos theme — [Cloud4Good: Spring '26](https://cloud4good.com/blog/salesforce-spring-26-release-key-updates-for-admins/) (snippet)
- **Reviewer verdict**: "cluttered and counterintuitive", "more clicks than it should"; "new features are layered on top of old ones", and the app "feels heavier and slower" — [Capterra Salesforce reviews 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/61368/Salesforce/reviews/?page=49) (snippet)

#### Close (deep)
- **Keyboard-first**: `?` shows all shortcuts and **`⌘/Ctrl+K` opens a command palette** (Smart Views, settings pages, org switching, lead creation). Global: ⌘⇧F focuses search, ⌘S collapses the sidebar, ⌘⇧L creates a lead, ⌘Enter submits a form, ⌘[ / ⌘] go back and forward. Lead page: ⌘F searches activities, ⌘⇧D calls, ⌘⇧K sends SMS, ⌘⇧E emails, ⌘⇧O adds a note, J/K moves to the next or previous lead from search results. Lists (Inbox, Search, Smart Views): J/K or arrows, Enter opens, ⌘Enter opens in a new window. Inbox: X check, Y/E done, R reply, A reply all, F forward, ⌘⇧Enter goes to the lead. Smart View: ⌘⇧X starts the dialer. Editor: `/` opens a quick-insert menu — [Close: keyboard shortcuts](https://help.close.com/docs/keyboard-shortcuts)
- **2025 "Sales Command Center" inbox**: "Lead & Opportunity context in every row" (status, value, due dates), "Potential Contacts" for unknown senders, hover insights, richer filters and visual priority indicators. Also new: Activity Smart Views (per call, email, SMS, WhatsApp, meeting, note), a central Saved Views page, an AI "Pipeline Guidance" view surfacing stuck or at-risk deals with suggested next actions, and email draft assist inside reminder notifications — [Close 2025 product roundup](https://close.com/es/blog/close-2025-product-roundup)
- **Lead page**: redesigned to load and navigate "40% faster", with **inline editing, split scrolling** and grouped SMS (date not confirmed; 2024-25) — [Close changelog](https://www.close.com/changelog/july-2024-improvements) (snippet). Old activities are visually de-emphasised so the feed scans faster (Jul 2024, older) — same source. **Pinned notes and custom activities** stick to the top of the activity feed (Aug 2025) — [Close changelog: pinned notes](https://www.close.com/changelog/pinned-notes-and-custom-activities)

#### Twenty (open source; deep, with real design tokens)
- **IA**: the sidebar can be reordered by drag, objects and views grouped in **folders**, unused objects hidden, **custom external links** added, and favorites pinned ("views, records, or searches"). It collapses via a button or the command menu. Views come as Table, Kanban and **Calendar**, each saving its own filters, sorts, field visibility and grouping, and each can be shared or private. **Clicking a record opens a right side panel**, and "Open" goes to the full page. **Record pages are tabs of widgets on a drag-and-resize grid** (fields, related records, emails, timeline, calendar, tasks, notes, files, charts, iFrames), editable via Cmd+K → "Edit record page layout" — [Twenty docs: layout overview](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/layout/overview)
- **Command menu**: Cmd/Ctrl+K to "perform actions, create records, and navigate" — [Twenty user guide](https://twenty.com/user-guide/section/getting-started/getting-around-twenty) (snippet)
- **Table**: show, hide, resize and drag-reorder columns. Grouping by a select field gives collapsible groups (sorted A-Z, Z-A or manually; hide groups via eye icons; "limit to 10-15 visible groups per view") — [Twenty docs: table views](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/views-pipelines/capabilities/table-views)
- **Kanban**: grouped by a Select field, with card fields toggled and reordered in Options → Fields. A **"Compact view"** hides all card fields. Column aggregates (Count, Sum, Average, Min, Max) are set by clicking the number next to the stage name — [Twenty docs: kanban views](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/views-pipelines/capabilities/kanban-views)
- **Footer aggregate operations available**: MIN, MAX, AVG, SUM, COUNT, COUNT_EMPTY, COUNT_NOT_EMPTY, COUNT_UNIQUE_VALUES, PERCENTAGE_EMPTY, PERCENTAGE_NOT_EMPTY, COUNT_TRUE, COUNT_FALSE — [Twenty source: AggregateOperations.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-front/src/modules/object-record/record-table/constants/AggregateOperations.ts)
- **Concrete design tokens** (the only top-tier modern CRM whose exact values are public):
  - Base `html { font-size: 13px }` (phones scale to 14/13), Inter, antialiased — [Twenty index.css](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-front/src/index.css)
  - Type scale: xs 0.85rem (about 11px), sm 0.92rem (about 12px), md 1rem (13px), lg 1.23rem (about 16px), xl 1.54rem (about 20px), xxl 1.85rem (about 24px). Weights 400/500/600 only; mono font DM Mono — [Twenty theme-light.css](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-ui/src/theme/theme-light.css)
  - Radii: 2, 4, 8, 16, 20 px plus pill. Text colors are gray at 0.2 (primary), 0.4 (secondary), 0.6 (tertiary) and 0.7 (light) lightness. Shadows are faint (e.g. 0.039 alpha) — [Twenty theme-light.css](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-ui/src/theme/theme-light.css)
  - Icons 14/16/20/24 px with strokes 1.6/2/2.5. Animation durations instant 75ms, fast 150ms, normal 300ms. Clickable background transition 0.1s. Table cell horizontal padding 8px, checkbox column 32px. **Side panel width 500px** — [Twenty ThemeCommon.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-ui/src/theme/constants/ThemeCommon.ts)
  - **Table row height 32px** — [Twenty RecordTableRowHeight.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-front/src/modules/object-record/record-table/constants/RecordTableRowHeight.ts). Column minimum width 104px — [Twenty RecordTableColumnMinWidth.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-front/src/modules/object-record/record-table/constants/RecordTableColumnMinWidth.ts)
  - A 12-step neutral gray scale from white to 0.2 — [Twenty GrayScaleLight.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-ui/src/theme/constants/GrayScaleLight.ts)

#### Affinity
- Objects are Persons, Organizations (Companies) and Opportunities, each with a profile page. **Lists** are spreadsheet-like collections of rows tied to one of those types — [Affinity developer docs: data model](https://developer.affinity.co/pages/data-model/the-basics)
- **Relationship strength** is a score from 10 to 100 based on recency and frequency, shown as **colored bars (green strong, orange average, gray weak)**. Profile tabs are Details, Lists/Opps, Notes, Connections and Activities. Connection types: People You Know, People Your Allies Know, Added Manually, and Inferred (shared work history) — [Affinity support: relationship strengths](https://support.affinity.co/s/article/Leveraging-your-Connections-and-Relationship-Strengths)
- 2025: global search preview, an AI chat beta ("ask your CRM anything in plain English"), and a hosted MCP server for Claude, Gemini and Copilot — [Affinity Q2 2025 release webinar](https://www.affinity.co/webinar/affinity-quarterly-product-release-webinar-2025-q2) (snippet); [Affinity: What's new](https://www.affinity.co/webinars/whats-new-in-affinity) (snippet)

#### Freshsales
- The detail page has an **action bar** for quick communication, **highlight cards** (lifecycle stage, recent conversations) and an activity timeline. **Freddy AI** writes summaries on contact and account pages (recent emails with sentiment, latest notes, associated deals). The kanban pipeline filters by owner and stage — [crm.org Freshsales review 2026](https://crm.org/news/freshworks-freshsales-crm-review) (snippet); [Freshworks product updates](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/sales/resources/product-updates) (snippet)

#### Streak
- Everything happens inside Gmail. Pipelines look like "a spreadsheet crossed with a kanban board", records are called "boxes", and it has Magic Columns, mail merge, snippets, tracking and a thread splitter. #### Clay (data workspace, not a CRM)
- Clay calls itself "a unified GTM database layer (Audiences)", not a CRM. #### Notion / Airtable as CRMs
- In Notion, records are **pages** (document-centric, narrative context). In Airtable they are **rows** (structured, relational), with grid, kanban, calendar and form views and **Interface Designer** for role-specific front-ends. Airtable templates ship multi-table CRMs with prebuilt interfaces and automations — [Routine: Notion vs Airtable CRM](https://routine.co/blog/posts/notion-airtable-crm-comparison) (snippet); [Fillout: Airtable vs Notion CRM](https://www.fillout.com/blog/notion-airtable-crm-database) (snippet)

### Inferences
- The "page = properties + rich body" model (Notion) and the "row = record" model (Airtable) both live on in Attio and Folk: the record page holds the narrative (notes, emails, timeline) and the table holds structure. A founder CRM should keep both and make moving between them instant (row → side panel → full page).
- Every leader has converged on the same view kit: Table + Kanban (+ Calendar in Twenty, Timeline and Calendar in Attio list views per the Hackceleration review) as **saved, named views over one dataset**, each with its own filters, sorts, visible fields and grouping.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch Attio's own pages describing the exact header/sidebar placement (left vs right) of its record page beyond the configuration doc; left main panel + right details sidebar is implied by "sidebar sections" but not stated verbatim.
- No authoritative source found for Folk's contact-page layout details (beyond "side peek", reminders and group views), its command palette, or a full shortcut list.

## Q1. Record page anatomy, and which layout reviewers like most

### Takeaway
The dominant 2026 anatomy is: **header** (avatar, name, a few key properties, quick actions, prev/next) → **highlights strip** (Attio up to 6, HubSpot header up to 5, Salesforce highlights up to 7) → **tabs** (Overview/Activity/Notes/Tasks/Emails/Files) in the main column → a **details sidebar** of editable properties, associations and list memberships. Lightweight products add a **peek/side panel** so a record can be opened without leaving the list. Reviewers punish clutter and wasted space and reward fast, scannable feeds (pinned items, de-emphasised history, split scrolling).

### Cited Findings
- **Attio**: tabs Overview/Activity/Notes/Tasks/Emails/Files, up to **6 highlight widgets** on Overview, a mandatory **Record Details** sidebar section (first 5 attributes by default), a **Lists** section, configurable relationship tabs and multiple Emails tabs, all configured per object — [Attio: Configure record pages](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/records/configure-record-pages)
- **HubSpot (2026 default)**: a two-column layout. The header has **up to 5 properties**, an avatar, two activity buttons, pagination arrows and breadcrumbs. Tabs are Overview (AI summary + Health), Activities, Revenue and Intelligence. The right sidebar holds Key information (inline edit), Associations (List/Chips) and Attachments (Focused) — [HubSpot KB: default record layout](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/understand-the-default-record-layout)
- **Salesforce**: a highlights panel driven by the compact layout (max 10 fields, first 7 shown), reused in hover cards and timeline expansions. Dynamic Forms place fields and sections as components with visibility rules — [Salesforce docs: compact layouts](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.210.0.salesforce1appadmin.meta/salesforce1appadmin/s1_admin_guide_compact_layouts_intro.htm) (older); [Atrium: Dynamic Forms](https://atrium.ai/resources/salesforce-dynamic-forms-beyond-drag-and-drop-design/)
- **Pipedrive**: a stage progress bar with days per stage, a left Summary sidebar (score, value, probability, expected close, person, org), composer tabs, then **Focus** (open activities, drafts, pinned notes) above **History**, plus a Changelog tab — [Pipedrive KB: deal detail view](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/deal-detail-view). The left-aligned redesign drew complaints about empty space on wide screens — [Pipedrive community](https://community.pipedrive.com/discussion/12010/new-details-screen-for-deals) (snippet; older)
- **Twenty**: a click opens a **500px right side panel**, and "Open" leads to the full page. The full page is **tabs of resizable widgets on a grid** (fields, related records, emails, timeline, calendar, tasks, notes, files, charts, iFrames) — [Twenty docs: layout](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/layout/overview); [Twenty ThemeCommon.ts](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/main/packages/twenty-ui/src/theme/constants/ThemeCommon.ts)
- **Folk**: "Side peek" opens a profile on the right while you browse on the left (2024, older) — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- **Close**: the redesigned lead page has inline editing and **split scrolling** (details and activity feed scroll independently) and is "40% faster" — [Close changelog](https://www.close.com/changelog/july-2024-improvements) (snippet). **Pinned notes** sit at the top of the feed (Aug 2025) — [Close: pinned notes](https://www.close.com/changelog/pinned-notes-and-custom-activities). Old activities are de-emphasised (2024, older) — [Close changelog Jul 2024](https://www.close.com/changelog/july-2024-improvements)
- **Affinity**: profile tabs Details, Lists/Opps, Notes, **Connections** and Activities, with relationship-strength bars — [Affinity support](https://support.affinity.co/s/article/Leveraging-your-Connections-and-Relationship-Strengths)
- **Freshsales**: an action bar, highlight cards and an activity timeline, with a Freddy AI summary on detail pages — [crm.org Freshsales review](https://crm.org/news/freshworks-freshsales-crm-review) (snippet)
- **AI summary placement**: HubSpot's Breeze summary card sits in the Overview tab and the hover Preview panel and auto-generates on load — [HubSpot KB: summarize records](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/summarize-records). Attio exposes summaries as an **AI attribute** (a field you can put in highlights or a table column) — [Attio: AI attributes](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-ai/ai-attributes) (snippet)

### Inferences
- The layout with the best reviewer signal is **two panes**: a main column of tabs and feed plus a narrow details sidebar, each scrolling independently (Close's split scrolling), with a capped highlights strip of 5-7 fields. Three-panel and left-crammed layouts (Pipedrive's wide-screen gap, Salesforce clutter) draw complaints.
- A **peek panel (~500px)** from tables and boards is now table stakes (Twenty, Folk, HubSpot Preview, Attio "Open preview" on kanban cards).
- "Focus vs History" (Pipedrive) and pinned notes (Close) are cheap, high-value devices for an agent-assisted CRM: open tasks, agent proposals and drafts belong in Focus, and the immutable log belongs in History.

### Gaps
- No quantitative reviewer survey compares record layouts directly; the "which layout is liked most" conclusion is inferred from scattered complaints and praise.
- No source states the pixel widths of Attio's or HubSpot's sidebars.

## Q2. How the best tables work

### Takeaway
Best-in-class tables (Attio, Twenty) behave like spreadsheets: dense 32px rows, inline cell editors, multi-cell selection with copy/paste to and from Excel or Sheets, footer aggregates per column, drag-reorderable and resizable columns, group-by on a select field, and filters, sorts and visible columns saved per named view. AI is arriving as **generated columns** (Attio AI attributes, Folk magic fields and AI Fields, Clay Claygent and enrichment columns, HubSpot smart properties) and **column insights** (HubSpot Breeze).

### Cited Findings
- Attio: inline editing; click-drag or shift+click multi-cell selection; copy/paste with external spreadsheets; arrow-key navigation with Enter to save; resize by dragging the header edge; relabel columns; relative-date and currency formatting; "+ Add calculation" footer per column — [Attio: table views](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/views/create-and-manage-table-views)

Collapsible groups by select field, with a recommended maximum of 10-15 visible groups — [Twenty: table views](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/views-pipelines/capabilities/table-views). Each view saves filters, sorts, field visibility and grouping and can be shared or private — [Twenty: layout](https://docs.twenty.com/user-guide/layout/overview)
- HubSpot 2026 index: view settings edited in place, **collapsible filter bar and summary-metric strip**, the index rendered as a report, **Breeze AI column insights**, and one button to add columns, create properties or create AI smart properties — [Vantagepoint: index redesign](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/hs/hubspot-smart-crm-index-redesign-guide). Associations render as "List" or "Chips" — [HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/understand-the-default-record-layout)
- Attio AI attributes: per-cell recalculate on hover, and column-header "Recalculate values with AI" — [Attio: AI attributes](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-ai/ai-attributes) (snippet)
- Performance as a feature: Attio filters and sorts 20k-50k+ records "within milliseconds" — [Hackceleration Attio review](https://www.hackceleration.com/labs/review/attio)
- Folk: fields are shown or hidden per view, and custom fields are scoped to a group, so each workflow's table carries only its own columns — [folk: data model](https://help.folk.app/en/articles/9790806-folk-data-model). Table views can be published as read-only links — [folk: share a view](https://help.folk.app/en/articles/6505030-share-a-view)

- Close: J/K row navigation, Enter to open, X to check, and ⌘⇧D/⌘⇧E to call or email the selected row from lists — [Close shortcuts](https://help.close.com/docs/keyboard-shortcuts)
- Clay: enrichment and AI agent columns as first-class column types — [Fast.io: Is Clay a CRM?](https://www.fast.io/resources/is-clay-a-crm)

### Inferences
- Minimum bar for a rebuilt table: 32px rows at a 13px base, a sticky header, the first (name) column pinned, inline editors by type (text, number, currency, date with relative display, select chips, multi-select chips, person avatar plus name, relation chips), a checkbox column for bulk actions, footer aggregates, J/K plus arrow navigation, copy/paste, and saved views.
- "Generated column" UX in practice means a toggle on the field definition, a visible per-cell regenerate affordance, and a column-level "recalculate all". In a human-plus-agent CRM the agent can be the engine behind these columns.

### Gaps
- Frozen-column and row-height (density) settings for Attio were not covered in the docs I fetched; I found no authoritative statement on whether Attio offers user-selectable row heights.

### Gaps

- I found no CRM with explicit WIP limits.

## Q7. New UI patterns of 2025-2026

### Takeaway

## Q8. Implications for a local, single-user CRM operated by a founder and an AI agent

### Takeaway
Copy the Attio/Twenty model (sidebar + saved views + record page with highlights, tabs and a details sidebar + Cmd+K) at Twenty-like density (13px Inter, 32px rows, 16px icons, short animations). Add the founder-specific affordances reviewers value: Folk-style fast capture and group-scoped fields, Close-style keyboard flow and pinned notes, Pipedrive-style staleness signals, and an explicit review queue for agent-proposed changes.

### Cited Findings
- Reviewers favour Folk for "relationship-driven founders" and Attio as "the best modern choice for product-led teams who think in data", while HubSpot remains the "safest default" — [Dupple: best CRM for startups 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/best-crm-for-startups) (snippet)
- Attio pain points to avoid: workflow setup that isn't intuitive, "3-4 clicks" between objects, and blank-slate confusion — [Hackceleration](https://www.hackceleration.com/labs/review/attio)
- Folk pain points to avoid: tasks and notes "hidden behind the core spreadsheet view" — [Whalesync](https://www.whalesync.com/blog/attio-vs-folk)
- Staleness signals: Attio time-in-stage turns red — [Attio kanban](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/views/create-and-manage-kanban-views); Pipedrive Rotting — [Pipedrive pipeline view](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/pipeline-view)
- Keyboard flow: Close's J/K, single-key inbox actions and ⌘K — [Close shortcuts](https://help.close.com/docs/keyboard-shortcuts)
- Agent review pattern: Pipedrive Nova proposes updates for review — [LetsDataScience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/pipedrive-launches-nova-ai-assistant-for-crm-updates-52a0540c) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Single-user scope removes most of the incumbents' complexity: no permissions UI, no per-team layouts, no share links. That frees room for keyboard depth and agent features.
- Suggested minimal object set: People, Companies, Deals (pipeline), Tasks, Notes, plus Lists/Groups (Folk-style) for workflows like investors, hiring or partners. Favorites should hold pinned views and records.
- Suggested record page: header (avatar, name, 3-5 highlight chips, actions: email, log, task, ask agent) → tabs Overview (AI summary + Focus: open tasks and pending agent proposals + pinned notes) / Activity / Notes / Tasks / Files → right sidebar (details, relations, lists, agent provenance), with split scrolling.

### Gaps
- No source evaluates CRM UX specifically for single-user, agent-co-operated setups; these recommendations are synthesis.
