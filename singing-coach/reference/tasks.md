# Tasks: Singing Coach

Every task cites what it implements. These features already exist in the published app `singing-coach` 1.0.0, so the build tasks are recorded as done.

## Build
- [x] T-1 (F-FRM-1, F-FRM-2, F-FRM-3): the sidebar. It has Practice, the places, badges for new songs and unread messages, today's minutes and voice against the cap, the light or rest day note, and the coach card with its status. It collapses to a remembered rail (forced on practice, editor, lesson and drill) and becomes a drawer on phones (`components/Sidebar.tsx`, `App.tsx`).
- [x] T-2 (F-FRM-4): "Practice" opens the next undone plan block (starting the plan), else the last song, else the Library (`goPractice`, `openBlock` in `App.tsx`).
- [x] T-3 (F-FRM-5): the quiet-instrument look: `--sc-*` tokens mixed from the kit's in light and dark, hairline panels with index and mono labels, mono readouts, the measuring grid, voice blue, and the black-and-white coach star, set in Inter and JetBrains Mono (`theme.css`, `components/ui.tsx`).
- [x] T-4 (F-FRM-6): the first-run dialog in four steps: Microphone, Timing, Your voice, Where to start (`components/Onboarding.tsx`).
- [x] T-5 (F-FRM-7): every page reports what the person is looking at (`app.ui`), for the coach's `app.context`.
- [x] T-6 (F-FRM-8): reload onto a new build after a reconnect or a tab show, waiting while a dialog is open (`lib/live.ts`).
- [x] T-7 (F-LIS-1, F-LIS-2, F-LIS-6): raw microphone capture with McLeod pitch in an AudioWorklet (main-thread fallback), clarity hysteresis, median smoothing and an octave guard. The microphone is asked for only on start, "Open in its own tab" appears when the frame blocks it, and it turns off when idle (`music/pitch.ts`, `music/mic.ts`).
- [x] T-8 (F-LIS-3, F-LIS-4, F-LIS-5): voice setup with the device picker, live meter, room noise and how you listen; the timing test with eight clicks; and the range test with comfortable range, family, clef, passaggi and range history (`components/VoiceSetup.tsx`, `voice.test`, `lib_voice.js`).
- [x] T-9 (F-DIAL-1, F-DIAL-2, F-DIAL-3): the Voice Dial on two canvases in song, hold, free, breath and blind modes, with the target and the voice's numbers beside it (`music/dial.ts`, `components/practice/VoiceDial.tsx`).
- [x] T-10 (F-PRC-1, F-PRC-2): the practice screen layout with the bar strip (sections by stage, bracket, drag and double-click), the pitch lane or sheet music with words, the dial, and the four modes on the audio clock (`pages/Practice.tsx`, `components/practice/BarStrip.tsx`, `music/lane.ts`, `music/sheet.ts`, `music/engine.ts`).
- [x] T-11 (F-PRC-3, F-PRC-4, F-PRC-14): the practice controls: verse, key shift with best fit, tempo, loop, metronome, count-in, drone, strictness and view. The guide is the formant voice, piano, start note or off, and the accompaniment is a written piano part or chord symbols. Keyboard shortcuts and the ask-the-coach box are on the same screen (`music/audio.ts`).
- [x] T-12 (F-PRC-5, F-PRC-6): note, phrase and take judgements and the result card, with no single score (`music/judge.ts`, `components/practice/ResultCard.tsx`).
- [x] T-13 (F-PRC-7, F-PRC-8): feedback that fades with the stage, the self-estimate before the numbers, and disputing a judged note (`runs.dispute`).
- [x] T-14 (F-PRC-9): "Stop: it hurts" ends practice and closes and marks the session (`sessions.end` with pain), making tomorrow gentle.
- [x] T-15 (F-PRC-10, F-PRC-11): pause and resume with sound, clock, microphone and recording stopping together. Mid-take changes take effect from that moment, and only mode, bars, tempo, key and verse restart the take.
- [x] T-16 (F-PRC-12, F-PRC-13): plan blocks counted on the practice screen, plus generated exercises (`?gen=`) and lesson examples (`?ex=`) whose takes move a skill (`music/generate.ts`).
- [x] T-17 (F-PRG-1, F-PRG-2, F-PRG-3): `runs.save` moves the covered sections per key, extends the session and suggests the next tempo. Sessions close after 20 quiet minutes, and reviews are asked for (`lib_runs.js`, job `sc_tick`).
- [x] T-18 (F-HLT-1, F-HLT-2, F-HLT-3, F-HLT-4, F-HLT-5): voice care. It covers load and caps by level, light, rest and ramp flags with the health banner, the voice check and s/z test, the four-week rule, and opt-in recordings with Delete all (`lib_health.js`, `components/CheckIn.tsx`, `health.checkin`, `runs.forget-audio`).
- [x] T-19 (F-TOD-1, F-TOD-2): the day's plan by the rules or the coach, in sittings, with Start and Continue and "Use the app's plan" (`lib_plan.js`, `plan.today|start|done|reset`, `pages/Today.tsx`).
- [x] T-20 (F-TOD-3, F-TOD-4, F-TOD-5): the Today dashboard: the week dial, streak and level ring; the numbers with deltas and sparklines; 30 days of voice; the voice map; songs in progress; the 12-week calendar; and the coach panel (`stats.home`, `components/charts.tsx`).
- [x] T-21 (F-LIB-1, F-LIB-2): Library shelves by level with contour tiles over the comfortable range, fit badges, stages, search and the six filters (`pages/Library.tsx`).
- [x] T-22 (F-LIB-3, F-LIB-4): the song panel with range in this key, best key and transpose, sections, history, trouble bars, last takes and gloss, plus Edit, Start over, Remove and Remove the recording (`components/library/SongPanel.tsx`, `stats.song`).
- [x] T-23 (F-LIB-5): the 41 starter items, all added on a fresh install and new ones after an update, each parsed before it is added (`lib_starter.js`, `ensureSeeded` in `lib_model.js`).
- [x] T-24 (F-ADD-1, F-ADD-2, F-ADD-3): Add song by asking the coach, writing ABC with the live check, or importing ABC, MusicXML (with a piano part kept as accompaniment) or UltraStar, with chords worked out when a file has none (`components/library/AddSong.tsx`, `NotationEditor.tsx`, `music/musicxml.ts`, `music/harmony.ts`, `songs.check`, `abc.parse`).
- [x] T-25 (F-REC-1, F-REC-2, F-REC-3, F-REC-5): a song from a recording. Demucs separates it in a worker (WebGPU, else WebAssembly), then the melody, key, beat, metre, bar 1 and chords are found, and Whisper hears the words with their times. The models come through `GET /api/models/part` in pinned 16 MB parts (`components/library/RecordingImport.tsx`, `music/separate.ts`, `music/stems.worker.ts`, `music/transcribe.worker.ts`, `music/models.ts`, `lib_models.js`).
- [x] T-26 (F-REC-4): the person checks the beat by ear, corrects metre, speed and bar 1, hears the voice and music apart, and corrects the words or pastes lyrics that keep the timing (`music/recording.ts`, `music/lyrics.ts`).
- [x] T-27 (F-REC-6, F-REC-7, F-REC-8): the recording, voice and music kept with the song (`songs.backing`) and played in Listen, Sing and Echo. The voice and the music each switch on or off, the piano takes over away from the recording's key and speed, and "Back to the recording" returns. Voice memos and unseparated mixes take the middle out (`music/backing.ts`, `music/opus.ts`).
- [x] T-28 (F-EDT-1, F-EDT-2, F-EDT-3, F-EDT-4): the song editor's piano roll with the chord lane and word lanes: note selection, moves, lengths, drawing, split, join, copy, delete and marks; word typing, dragging and shifting; verses; and chords on the beat (`pages/Editor.tsx`, `components/editor/Roll.tsx`, `music/songdoc.ts`).
- [x] T-29 (F-EDT-5, F-EDT-6, F-EDT-9): key, whole-song transposition, metre, pickup, tempo and details beside the roll, playback from any bar with the recording, and the notation as text (`music/editplay.ts`).
- [x] T-30 (F-EDT-7, F-EDT-8, F-EDT-10, F-EDT-11): undo and redo with autosave through `songs.update`, held while `songs.check` finds problems. Navigation by strip, wheel, hand tool and arrows, the "changed elsewhere" check, and the exact round trip of the notation (`musicxml.writeSong`).
- [x] T-31 (F-CCH-1, F-CCH-2, F-CCH-3, F-CCH-4, F-AGT-3, F-AGT-7): the coach conversation with live request status and Ask again, songs added under the reply, and asking from Coach, Today, practice and Add song. It comes with the `coach_message` playbook and `coach.unsend` (`pages/Coach.tsx`, `lib_coach.js`).
- [x] T-32 (F-LRN-1, F-LRN-2, F-LRN-3): 80 lessons in 13 units with sung and answered tasks, resume and score, and the ten-level path with automatic level-up (`pages/Learn.tsx`, `learn/LessonPlayer.tsx`, `learn/content/`, `lib_curriculum.js`, `lib_learn.js`).
- [x] T-33 (F-TRN-1, F-TRN-2, F-TRN-3): Train with 41 skills, mastery and stars, quiz, sing and speak drills, 33 vocalises in the singer's range, and sight-singing in 30 levels (`pages/Train.tsx`, `learn/Drill.tsx`, `learn/drills.ts`, `drills.save`).
- [x] T-34 (F-REF-1, F-REF-2, F-REF-3, F-REF-4, F-REF-5): Reference tabs: tuner, pitch pipe and drone; voice types; intervals; solfège and hand signs; vowels; vocalises; tone lab; glossary; voice care (`pages/Reference.tsx`, `music/tone.ts`).
- [x] T-35 (F-SET-1, F-SET-2, F-SET-3, F-SET-4, F-SET-5, F-SET-6, F-SET-7): Settings in seven panels, saving on change, with data export as ABC, UltraStar and session-log CSV (`pages/Settings.tsx`, `settings.update`).
